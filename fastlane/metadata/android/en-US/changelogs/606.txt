0.6.6: The Market, and Folio in Chinese.

* The Folio Market: the app icon opens a store with Featured, Sources, Packages, Installed and Settings. For supporters first; everyone gets it in 0.7.0. Every theme and tweak it hands out is still in Settings for everyone else.
* Add a source: any HTTPS address. Folio shows its key fingerprint before you trust it, and says plainly what a changed key looks like.
* Nothing is applied unchecked: size, checksum and signature first, then the whole package in one go, with Undo beside it.
* Folio speaks Simplified Chinese, and a renamed Chinese app answers to its pinyin in Spotlight and the App Library.
