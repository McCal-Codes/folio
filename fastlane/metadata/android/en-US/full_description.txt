Folio Launcher: a clean, iPhone-style Home Screen for Android, with the jailbreak tweaks I always wanted, and none of the lockdown.

Status: very early. It's a bit rusty in places, and things will settle down as more people use it. Developed and tested on a Galaxy Z Fold8.

Adapts to the screen, doesn't just stretch
* Lays out by how much room there is, not what device it is: phones, flips, foldables, split-screen, tablets.
* Open the Fold and you get two Home pages side by side. Portrait unfolded gets one centered page with a dock bar. The cover in landscape gets two columns.
* Knows where the fold is: when the phone is half folded, sheets, menus and Home rows move off the crease.
* Bigger screens draw everything bigger, like iPad, instead of a tiny phone layout in the middle.

The iOS stuff
* Side Bar with the status bar, a Dynamic Island that wraps the camera, and the dock.
* Notification Center, Control Center, Spotlight, Today View, Smart Stacks and the widget gallery.
* Jiggle mode, folders, Icon Stacks, per-page icon size and labels, and App Library search.
* Focus modes with schedules and the Home pages you want to see.
* Settings built like iOS: menus with checkmarks, segmented controls, switches, sheets and alerts.

Tweaks (you pick which ones you get)
* Ideas from jailbreak tweaks I liked (Velox, Activator, Velvet, Axon, ColorFlow, Harbor), rebuilt from scratch. None of their code is in here, and everyone is credited in the app.
* Get the ones you want from the Tweak Library, Sileo-style. Only those show up in Settings, and you can remove them any time.
* Turn a tweak on for just the cover or just the inner screen, and Safe Mode if something crashes.

Themes
* Save your look as a theme file, import one, or share a theme straight to Folio from Files or Chrome.
* Pick an app icon, and live Clock and Calendar icons that match your other icons.

Private
* No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Folio itself doesn't send anything anywhere unless you share a crash report or check for updates, which only asks GitHub for the latest release.
* Google search, Discover and widgets from other apps use those apps' own services.
* Every permission is optional and explained where it's used, and listed in PERMISSIONS.md with what happens if you say no.

Free and open source under the MIT licence. Based on DuoLauncher by jakesgoodapps, rebuilt for Compose.
