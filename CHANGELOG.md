# Changelog

All notable changes to Folio. Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org) (MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH; 0.x while
Folio is in development), and this file follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com). The app's
`versionCode` is derived from the version name ((MAJOR × 10000 + MINOR × 100 + PATCH) × 10 + HOTFIX), so every release sorts correctly and a fix on top of one has nine numbers of its own.
Folio shows the newest section on the phone after an update, and every version under Settings › What's New › Version History.

## [0.6.9] - Unreleased

### Added
- **Crackgate joke:** Settings › Fold & Displays › Duet has a "Crackgate joke" switch, off to start with. With it on, "Show me" draws a pretend Duo hinge fault (a bright bar or line down and across the screen, in cyan or green, never red) over the screen to show friends; shake the phone to clear it. It needs Folio's gestures turned on and shows over other apps only, not over Folio's own Home. It fades in once, "A little glitchy" (on to start with) adds a few slow changes, and with Reduce Motion it just appears and stays still. Nothing flashes, nothing makes a sound, and it clears itself after 10 minutes or when the screen turns off and is never saved.
- **Folio tiles in Quick Settings:** two tiles, Folio Notifications and Folio Control Center, can be added to the phone's Quick Settings and open Folio's panels over the open app (they need Folio's gestures turned on and Folio's panels over other apps, as the swipe down does); and Folio's Notification Center has a button for the phone's Quick Settings.
- **Pick which panels are Folio's:** with the pull down from the top of any app turned on, Settings › Notifications › Panels now has "Swipe down opens": both of Folio's panels (the start), Folio notifications or Folio Control Center (the other half then opens the phone's own).
- **Switch between panels:** two icons in a pill at the top of each panel open the other one, sliding across (a cross-fade with Reduce Motion), so you do not close and pull down again. TalkBack names both. For supporters and Folio Dev for now, and for everyone in 0.7.0.
- **Move controls in Control Center:** in edit mode, press and hold a control, drag it onto another and let go to put it there. A drop on empty space puts it back, so nothing moves by itself; TalkBack gets Move earlier and Move later. For supporters and Folio Dev for now, and for everyone in 0.7.0.
- **AirPods battery without root:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, Settings › Dynamic Island has an AirPods battery card with a switch, "AirPods battery (uses Bluetooth scan)", off to start with. Turned on, and with Android's Nearby devices permission (asked for right there), Folio listens for at most 5 seconds to the battery your AirPods broadcast while they are connected and the headphones card or that card is on screen, at most once every 30 seconds, and shows left, right and case in steps of 10% on the headphones card and in Settings, with where the reading came from. Other people's AirPods are ignored, nothing is stored or sent, and the permission is never used for location. This release asks for one new permission, Nearby devices (`BLUETOOTH_SCAN`), only when you turn the switch on; see PERMISSIONS.md.
- **Storage:** Settings › General › Storage shows how full the phone is, one row for each USB or SD drive that is connected, the five biggest apps when Usage Access is on, and shortcuts to Android's own Free up space, Storage settings, Files and Downloads screens. It only reads sizes and opens Android's screens; it deletes and moves nothing. It is off for everyone except supporters on the beta and Folio Dev until a later release.
- **Control Center look:** Settings › Notifications › Panels › Control Center look chooses how the tiles are drawn: Clear glass (see-through, with a bright edge), Frosted (lighter tiles on a darker plate that keeps text readable over any app, the start) or Solid (opaque, as before).
- **AirPods battery with root:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, on a phone where root works in Settings › Advanced › System Bridge, an AirPods battery card reads the left, right and case battery that Android's Bluetooth service already holds, when you tap Refresh or, with "Show on the headphones card" on, when the headphones card appears. It runs one fixed read-only command at most every 30 seconds, asks for no new permission, keeps and sends nothing, and says in words when Android has no numbers or root is gone. Copy AirPods report puts a redacted copy of the relevant lines on the clipboard so the reading can be corrected from a real phone.
- **More Control Center settings:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, Settings > Notifications & Control Center has short cards for Control Center: Pages (switches for the Media and sound and Connectivity pages, and Open on Controls or the last page used), Sliders (haptic ticks while dragging), Controls (an Edit controls row that opens Control Center in edit mode, and Close after a toggle, off to start with) and Reset Control Center, which keeps your controls and offers Undo for a few seconds. The brightness slider gets an Auto chip, so you can see that dragging turned Auto brightness off and turn it back on.
- **Adjust the island:** Settings › Dynamic Island › Adjust island shows the island on a dimmed screen with a round handle to make it wider, arrows to nudge it into place, Fit to camera (when Android reports a wider camera than the lens the island wraps) and Reset. A phone set to a smaller minimum width can leave the island narrower than its camera area; this is the way to fix it. The width is saved per screen and orientation, the island on Home and in other apps uses it, and TalkBack gets Wider, Narrower and four Move actions. Behind the beta gate for now.
- **Advanced island:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, and for everyone in 0.7.0. Settings › Dynamic Island › Advanced island (below Adjust island) is a page of five short lists for power users: Color (Black, Graphite, Navy, Plum or a hex code of your own), Corner radius (Round, Soft, Squarer), Animation speed (Slower, Standard, Faster), Haptics (Off, Light, Standard) and Gestures (what Tap, Hold, Swipe up and Double tap do: Expand, Open the app, Dismiss or Nothing), with Reset all at the top. Every option starts as the island is today. The color is always solid, text and icons switch between white and black to stay readable, a custom color that would be hard to read is moved until it is (and the page says so), and the island stays black while it wraps a real camera hole so the lens never shows a lighter ring. Corners are never squarer than the camera hole's own curve, speed is capped so a change never reads as a flicker and Reduce Motion still wins, haptics follow Android's touch feedback switch, and one gesture always stays on Dismiss. Double tap makes a single tap wait the system's double-tap time, and only while it is set to something. Until it opens, the island stays exactly as it is today.
- **Control Center without repeats:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, the Media and Connectivity pages show only when the first page cannot hold what they hold (a short window, a big font size or many controls), so on a roomy screen the strip has no pages that repeat the first. While nothing is playing, Now Playing is one slim row with the title instead of a full card with dead buttons, without anything else on the page moving when playback starts. The battery ring stands clear of the page dots.
- **Music widget:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, Folio's own now-playing widget, added from the widget gallery under Folio. It is a slim Strip when it is short, a Card in between and a Poster with a blurred backdrop and a wave when there is room, and it reads the track from your player the same way the island does. Play, pause, previous and next are 48 dp touch targets, times show only when the player gives a duration, and the wave freezes when paused or with Reduce Motion. With nothing playing it says Not Playing in the same three shapes. (Control Center's Now Playing tile also has a scrubber with times and a spoken description, for everyone.)
- **Hinge detents:** for supporters and Folio Dev first, feel the Fold click as it opens and closes, with soft ticks at 45°, 90° and 135° and a firmer click when it lies flat. Light, Medium or Firm, in the Fold effect settings. With the optional root hinge angle you get every stop; without root Folio ticks once each time the Fold changes position. It replaces the single tick Folio played at halfway, and it is silent when Haptic Feedback is off.
- **Smart clock ink:** with Android's own wallpaper behind Home, Settings › Wallpaper & Appearance › Text on Home has a Smart clock ink switch, off to start with. It turns on its own accessibility service, separate from Folio's gestures service. With it on, the Big Clock takes a picture of Home only while Folio's Home is in front, measures the part under the clock, throws the picture away at once and picks light or dark text from what is really behind it. Only a few brightness numbers are kept; nothing is saved or sent.
- **Telling same-name apps apart in setup:** when two apps share a name on the Your Home apps page (two Calendars, say), each row shows its package name underneath so you know which one you are ticking.
- **Pick your Home apps in setup:** first setup has a new Your Home apps page after the starting point. It shows Home's grid live as you tick apps, with up to eight everyday apps ticked, one for each job Folio finds on the phone (phone, messages, camera, browser, photos, maps, calendar, Settings, and so on), and every other app under Show all apps. Nothing is applied until Continue. The first page also has Use Folio now, which puts the starter apps on Home, in place of the apps Folio first placed there, and goes straight to Make Folio Your Home.
- **Two more Control Center sizes:** Settings › Notifications › Control Center size now has Small and Extra Large beside Compact, Standard and Large, for tiny cover screens and big ones. Every size is checked to fit without scrolling.
- **Tiles you can feel:** a Control Center tile dips a little under your finger and springs back, and a slider shows its percent while you drag it. With Reduce Motion the tile only dims.
- **Control Center look:** Settings › Notifications › Panels › Control Center look chooses how the tiles are drawn: Clear glass (the start: see-through with a bright edge, as light as the picture behind it allows and darker only as far as white text needs), Frosted (smoky dark tiles that keep text readable over any wallpaper or app, with no plate behind them) or Solid (opaque, as before).
- **Blur behind panels on any phone:** over another app on a phone whose system will not blur windows (One UI turns it off), Folio's panels now sit on a blurred picture of that app: one screenshot is taken before the panel appears, shrunk at once, blurred once off the main thread and faded in, so nothing is blurred frame by frame and it stays smooth. The picture stays in memory, is never saved or sent, and is dropped when the panel closes; Blur at zero or Reduce Transparency turns it off, and a frosted glass in your wallpaper's colors stands in while it loads or if the phone refuses. This uses the screenshot capability, so turn Folio's gestures service off and on once after updating, and the service's description says so.
- **Tap the status rail for Control Center:** on Home, a tap on the Side Bar's status opens Control Center, as the iPhone Duo's status control does. It is off while Home is being edited or an icon is dragged, and TalkBack says what it opens.
- **Pull down from any app:** Settings › Notifications › Panels › Pull down from the top of any app, off to start with and available once Folio's panels over other apps is on. A pull down on the left half of an app's top edge opens Notification Center and on the right half Control Center, in place of the phone's shade, and a diagonal pull from the corner counts, which helps one-handed reach. A thin strip the height of the status bar (24 to 48 dp) takes the touches there while it is on; full screen apps and Home keep their own.
- **Control Center fills the screen:** instead of a corner panel, Control Center spreads across the window in pairs of columns (4, 6 or 8, never more than your controls can use), with cells close to the size you chose, and splits into three pages: Controls, Media and sound, and Connectivity. A strip of page buttons beside the grid (in the header on a short window) moves between pages, and a swipe down does too. Sizes come from the window, your size choice and the touch target, not from a device, so a phone on its side, a cover screen and the unfolded screen each fit.
- **Island status:** Settings › Dynamic Island now opens with a card that says why the island is or isn't showing over other apps (the gestures service, notification access, Do Not Disturb, or Android's own pop-ups taking over message alerts) and puts the fix one tap away.
- **Home spaced like the iPhone Duo:** for beta testers first, rows sit a little further apart where the window has spare height, and the dock apps are a little larger. It stays off for everyone else until 0.6.9 is released; nothing changes without it.
- **Smoother reply box and widget resize:** the small box you type a reply in over another app rises over the app and settles back down when you send, tap outside or press Back, and only fades with Reduce Motion. Resizing a widget over apps shows them sliding out of the way as you drag the handle, and sliding back if you cancel. In Notification Center, sections and Clear also follow Reduce Motion.
- **Visible actions in Notification Center:** with Notification sections on, every notification has a … button for the same menu as a long press or a swipe (Open, Snooze, Notification settings, Clear), and an app's expanded list has a Clear that asks first, then clears only that app's clearable notifications. Nothing needs a gesture any more; the gestures stay as shortcuts.
- **Notification sections:** Settings › Notifications › Panels › Notification sections, off to start with, sorts Notification Center into Now (music, a timer running), Conversations, the rest, and a Quiet section folded away with its count, for notifications whose channel Android marks as silent. A call, an alarm, a timer, a conversation or a channel that bypasses Do Not Disturb is never put in Quiet, and a notification Android hasn't ranked yet stays in the main pile. Clear all still clears Quiet too. Over other apps, the panels show the same sections.
- **Pick more rows by hand:** Settings › Home › Rows now offers 5, 6 and 7 as well as 4 and Automatic. A count that doesn't fit at the usual size first tightens the space under labels, then makes icons smaller, down to 48 dp, instead of making the page scroll, so a tall screen like the Fold8 Ultra's can show seven rows. Both screens use the same number, so a screen with less room shows smaller icons. Automatic and 4 are unchanged.
- **Home settles when the layout changes:** after a widget move or resize that pushes apps along, an Undo, or a new Rows setting, apps and icons slide to their new places instead of jumping. Folding the phone still places them at once, and Reduce Motion and Animation Speed apply as everywhere.
- **Clear Suggestions History:** Settings › Search & App Library › Suggestions has a Clear Suggestions History row. After you confirm, Folio forgets what it learned from your app use (Spotlight suggestions, the App Library tile, the Suggestions widget, Smart Stack relevance) and empties your recent apps. Android's own usage data is not changed; Folio just ignores anything from before you cleared.
- **Reduce Motion:** Settings › Accessibility › Reduce Motion is a step between all motion and Android's Remove animations, like iOS. Pages, sheets, menus and alerts fade in where they land instead of sliding or growing, the install check fades in instead of popping, icons stop wiggling while you edit Home, and switches stop without bouncing. Remove animations turns it on too. Animation Speed still applies.
- **Press and lift on icons:** with the new motion on (supporters first), apps, dock icons and folders all dip the same way under your finger, an icon lifts with a soft shadow while its menu is open, and after a tap it stays dipped until the app is up (half a second at most), so a slow app no longer looks like nothing happened. With Reduce Motion on the icon only dims.
- **Pages that stretch, catch and land:** with the new motion on, dragging past Home's first page or the App Library stretches with less and less give and springs back, like iPhone. Touching a page that is still moving stops it under your finger without opening the app there, and a flick lands with a small bounce. The first page keeps its stop when Discover opens from there. With Reduce Motion on the pages stop at the ends as before, and catching still works.
- **Performance log by what Folio was doing:** besides the whole run, the report gives the frame timing (p50, p95, p99 and janky frames) of the fold animation, Home swipes, folders opening, widget resizing, pages, sheets, menus and alerts opening, and moving an icon on Home, each on their own, so a slow one shows up by name.
- **Copy Diagnostics beside Share:** Settings › General › Advanced › Diagnostics has Copy Diagnostics next to Share Diagnostics, so the report can be pasted into a form or a chat without going through a share sheet. It is the same text a shared report holds, and nothing leaves your phone.
- **Capabilities and Recent Activity:** Settings › General › Advanced › Diagnostics shows what Folio can use on this phone (Standard, Notification access and Accessibility, each On or Off, and what it is for), with a button to Android's settings for the ones that are off, and Folio's own recent activity, newest first, with a Failed filter for problems it carried on from. Both are read-only and stay on the phone.
- **Suggestions, any size:** the Suggestions widget now goes from one cell to the whole grid. Its size menu adds 1 × 1, Row (four across) and Column (four down), and the icons keep to the size of your Home icons instead of stretching to fill the card.
- **Your own picture for an app's icon:** long-press an app, More › Edit Icon › Your Picture › Choose Picture, and one icon shows a photo you pick, cropped to a square. Remove Picture or Reset Icon puts the normal icon back. The picture is copied into Folio, so it keeps working if the photo is deleted; it stays on this phone and is not part of a backup.
- **Focus that turns on by itself:** each Focus has an Also Turn On When card with Folding (Unfolded, Cover Screen or Tent), Charging and Headphones. When one holds, the Focus turns on, the list says why, and it turns off again when the signal ends. If you turn it off yourself, it stays off until the signal changes. For supporters and Folio Dev for now, and for everyone in 0.6.9.
- **Drag the dock in edit mode:** with the dock on the Side Bar, a handle under it lets you drag the dock up or down instead of using the Dock Height slider, and the handle stays on screen. For supporters and Folio Dev for now, and for everyone in 0.6.9.

The first time the Market opens it asks once, "Keep your packages up to date?", and Not Now is remembered and never asked again. Pick a Look (Classic, Thin, Bold, Tinted, Soft, Editorial or Stacked, with the hours over the minutes), then a color from one row: Automatic, a tint of your picture, the picture's own colors that stay readable, or White. A swapped app still opens the real app, and its widgets and shortcuts are hidden while the mode is on. Apps that came with the phone are never swapped, the list stays on your phone, and it isn’t part of a backup.

- **Badges that pop:** when a notification count first appears on an icon or folder, the badge pops in once from a little smaller, with a tiny bounce. - **A switch with a little squish:** the thumb of a switch widens a touch while you press it and while it travels, then settles. With Reduce Motion on it settles without bouncing. Supporters on the beta see it first.

- **Pages and sheets leave as they came:** with the new motion on, Settings and the Market slide back out when you close them with Done or Back, a form sheet on the inner screen drops back down when you tap outside it, and an alert shrinks a little as it fades when you tap outside it. With Reduce Motion on they fade out instead.

### Changed
- **The Roadmap, rewritten:** the 0.6.9 section groups what is in the betas into fewer, clearer rows and adds what is still being built (Setup's Your Home apps page, Duo-style spacing, short windows, the missing-widget tile); 0.7.0 now lists StandBy 2, Spotlight Pro, the Music widget and a public Keyd page; Simple Mode and presets have their own 0.7.2 section; Later names the held ideas honestly.
- **Home spaced like iPhone Duo (supporters in the betas, everyone at 0.6.9):** on a phone-sized screen such as the cover, the four rows spread out toward the Duo's row spacing when the window has free height (up to 20 percent more room per row, taken only from height that is free), and the dock's apps grow from 72 to 80 percent of Home's icons wherever the dock has room. Rows 5 to 7, short or sideways windows, the unfolded screen and tablets keep their spacing, and no row or column is ever lost.
- **New motion moved to Accessibility:** the New motion switch (supporters and Folio Dev) is now in Settings › Accessibility, next to Reduce Motion, instead of under General › Advanced. Searching Settings for "motion" finds it there.
