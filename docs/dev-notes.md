# Folio Dev notes: steps to try in this build

Optional. Each "- " line becomes a checkbox on Folio Dev's "New build" page (Settings › General › What's New › This build).
Leave the file out or empty and the page still shows what changed. Keep this list short and about the build in hand; replace
it for each beta (the 0.6.9 beta 1 list below mirrors Settings › Help › What to Test).

- Settings > Home Screen & Dock: switch Bottom of Home between Search button, Page dots and Nothing; Home should not shift
- Settings > Icons & Side Bar > Status: turn on Stronger rings on the cover and the inner screen
- Put a widget at the top of a page: the rows below it are evenly spaced and the dock lines up
- Edit Home with the dock on the Side Bar: drag the handle under the dock
- Focus > Also Turn On When: Charging turns a Focus on and off; turned off by hand it stays off
- Market > Settings: nothing is on to start with; the first time the Market opens it asks once
- Market > Installed: Updated recently lists automatic updates, and Undo puts the earlier version back
- Settings > Help > What to Test: tick through the list
