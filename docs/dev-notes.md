# Folio Dev notes: steps to try in this build

Optional. Each "- " line becomes a checkbox on Folio Dev's "New build" page (Settings > General > What's New > This build).
Leave the file out or empty and the page still shows what changed. Keep this list short and about the build in hand; replace
it for each beta (the 0.6.9 beta 6 list below mirrors Settings > Help > What to Test). Folio Dev opens every gate, so the gated
lines at the end can be tried here. Lines marked "Fold" need a person on the Fold. Lines marked "restore" change a phone setting.

- Control Center (tap the status rail on Home): swipe down from a gap between tiles; the next page follows your finger, swipe up returns without closing
- Control Center, first page: drag up from empty space; the panel follows, ticks at the close point and closes, a short slow drag springs back
- Control Center: tap the close button beside power, then tap the empty margin around the grid; both close it, a tap between two tiles keeps it open
- Home status rail: hold the status capsule; only the capsule shades, with no faint second pill above it
- Control Center with music playing: press the volume keys; the volume slider moves with each press
- Settings > Notifications & Control Center: step Control Center size from Small to Extra Large; nothing scrolls or clips
- Open a folder on Home (cover and inner, Fold): Home behind it is blurred and dimmed, and none of its text shows behind the title
- Home, rotate to landscape and back to portrait a few times (restore rotation): every row is whole above the Search strip and Folio stays open
- Android Settings > Apps, Turn off an app that is on Home, then Turn on (restore): its icon keeps its spot, dimmed while off
- Android Settings > Display > Font size at the largest (restore): Home labels, Spotlight chips, Folio Settings and Market tab labels are not cut off
- Settings > Wallpaper & Appearance > Text on Home: turn on Smart clock ink with Android's own wallpaper; the Big Clock text follows what is behind it
- Settings > Help > What to Test > Open the New Setup: the Your Home apps page shows the grid as you tick; Skip changes nothing
- Gated, Control Center without repeats (use the inner screen, Fold): no Media or Connectivity page that repeats the first; with nothing playing, Now Playing is one slim row
- Gated, Settings > Notifications & Control Center > Pages: turn Media and sound page off and set Open on to Last page used; Control Center follows
- Gated, Control Center brightness slider (restore): tap the Auto chip; it turns green and drags turn it off
- Gated, Settings > Notifications & Control Center > Controls: Edit controls opens Control Center in edit mode; Close after a toggle closes it on a flashlight tap
- Gated, Settings > Notifications & Control Center > Reset Control Center: size, look and options return to defaults and Undo brings them back
- Gated, Settings > Dynamic Island > Adjust island (cover and inner, Fold): drag the handle, use the arrows, Fit to camera, Reset, Done
- Gated, Home on the cover and inner screen (Fold): rows spread like the iPhone Duo, dock apps larger, every row visible
- Gated, Settings > Fold & Displays > Hinge detents (Fold): pick Light, Medium and Firm and open the Fold slowly; ticks at 45, 90 and 135 degrees and a click at flat
- Gated, Edit Home > Add widget > Folio > Music: add Strip, Card and Poster with a player playing; the controls and scrubber work
- Settings > Help > What to Test: tick through the list
