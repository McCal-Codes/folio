# Folio 0.7.0 beta 3

*Written from McCal's own words on 11 Nov 2026. Released 11 Nov 2026 as a pre-release on folio-beta.*

Beta 3 is mostly about the dock, and about making it hard to get yourself into trouble.

## What's new

- **More apps, never overflow.** The dock can hold up to eight places on a tall enough screen. On a short screen it shows fewer instead of spilling off the edge. If you want more apps to fit, there is an optional **Fit more dock apps** switch in Dock settings that lets icons get smaller than the usual touch size. It is off by default, because I would rather you choose that than have it happen to you.
- **Your dock stays safe.** Anything beyond six places is kept beside your layout, so going back to an older version never breaks your dock, and coming forward again brings the rest back.
- **Dock size.** Settings › Home Screen & Dock › Dock › Size makes the dock's apps smaller or larger, and **Center on the side** keeps a side dock centered.
- **Beta access at a glance.** Settings › Supporter has a card that tells you where you stand on betas. Some of you could not tell why you were not getting new ones, and this should answer it.
- **Older versions.** Software Update has a place for going back to an older version. It is a safety net for the future. I have not published an older build yet, so the list is empty for now.
- **Widgets are managed from Edit on Home.** The widget list in Settings is gone, since Home already knows what is on it.

## Please try first

The Beta access card in Settings › Supporter. Check that it matches where you think you stand, and tell me if it does not.

## About the betas

Anyone can see the beta. To use it, you get me a coffee on Ko-fi. I try not to lock anyone out of features, and this is the closest way I can get support for the time I put into Folio. Or you can wait a month or so for the next release and get everything then.

Even with the betas, I will fix issues without you buying a coffee, no matter what. Keep reporting!
