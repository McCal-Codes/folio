Version `0.6.9-beta.5`, versionCode 6090 like the earlier betas: a beta shares its release's code, and Software Update orders
betas by name, so beta.5 installs over beta.4 and the betas before it. Supporters get it through Market > Sources >
Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.4

- **Folio's panels over other apps:** Settings > Notifications > Panels has a Folio's panels over other apps switch, off to start with.
  When One UI's one-hand operation or a Routine opens the notification shade or Quick Settings, Folio draws its own Notification Center
  or Control Center over the open app. A tap outside, a swipe up, going Home or leaving it alone closes it. Reply opens a small box
  to type in. Options and the ... button are on Home only.
- **Notification Center layouts:** an Unfolded layout choice replaces the old clock-beside-notifications switch: Full screen, a Card
  under the corner you pulled from, or Side by side with Control Center on one page.
- **Notification sections:** off to start with. Now (music, a timer), Conversations, the rest, and a Quiet section folded away with its
  count. A call, an alarm, a timer, a conversation or a channel that bypasses Do Not Disturb never goes in Quiet.
- **A ... button and Clear per app:** with sections on, every notification has a ... button for the same menu as a long press, and an
  app's list has a Clear that asks first.
- **Pick 5, 6 or 7 rows:** Settings > Home > Rows. Icons get smaller to fit instead of the page scrolling, down to 48 dp.
- **Widgets make room:** moving or resizing a widget over apps pushes them along, on to a new page if they have to. Undo puts it back.
- **Add to Home from Spotlight:** the Top Hit has a + button, and says where an app already is.
- **Smoother changes:** apps and icons slide to their new places after a widget move, an Undo or a new Rows setting, and the apps slide
  out of the way live while you resize a widget. The reply box rises over the app and settles back down. The new panels and sections follow
  Animation Speed and Reduce Motion.
- **Island status:** Settings > Dynamic Island opens with a card that says why the island is or isn't showing over other apps (the
  gestures service, notification access, Do Not Disturb, or Android's own pop-ups taking over message alerts) and puts the fix one tap away.
- **Settings regrouped:** Reduce Transparency sits beside Reduce Motion in Accessibility. On Layout the dock's Placement menu joined the
  card with its Width, Space between dock apps, Align and Height, and that card is now called Dock. The two Background rows are now
  Folder background and Status bar background.

## Fixes

- **The Focus pill fades with Reduce Motion:** the pill that shows the active Focus at the top of Home slid in and out even with Reduce Motion on. It now fades where it lands.
- **Button Bar labels are translated:** the Recent apps, Home and Back buttons of the on-screen Button Bar are read out in your language, for TalkBack and for the buttons.
- **Arrows face the right way in right-to-left languages:** the back and disclosure arrows in Settings rows and the Market back arrow flip in Arabic, Hebrew and other right-to-left languages.
- **The Big Clock reads the picture as it looks on Home:** with Dark appearance dims wallpaper on, or Darken behind text drawing its gradient at the top, the clock now counts the dim and the gradient when it picks its text color. Its soft shade is also only as strong as the picture needs, up to about 54%, instead of always 40%.
- **Find Folio's own widgets by search:** the widget gallery's search finds Clock, Date, Big Clock and the rest by name (or "Folio"), and "No Widgets Found" no longer shows under Folio's own widgets.
- **Layout backups:** a widget whose app is missing is left out (with a count) instead of stopping the backup, a blank app, shortcut or widget name no longer makes a backup that can't be restored, and a failed backup says why in plain words.
- **Cancelled installs and expired codes:** tapping Cancel on Android's install prompt is no longer shown as a failure, a supporter link can replace a code that has run out, and "Folio was updated" only appears after updates Folio installed on its own.
- **Package files and old links:** a package from a slow source stops at four seconds instead of hanging and says why it can't open one. Older supporter links that start with folio://early redeem like the newer ones.
- **Control Center over another app no longer closes Folio:** with Folio's panels over other apps on, the Folio Quick Settings shortcut (or a swipe that opens Control Center over an app) closed Folio because Control Center listened for Back in a window that has no Back. It now skips that where there is none.
- **The Dynamic Island no longer risks a crash when notifications are swiped away:** its two short-term memories of what already popped up were used from two threads at once, and a chat app clearing a burst of notifications could corrupt them. They now take a lock.

## For people with a phone that restricts launchers

- The troubleshooting guide has a new section for Xiaomi and HyperOS: gesture navigation switching to buttons, the phone's own shade
  showing on a swipe down, and the gesture handle taking room.

## Not in this beta

Smart clock ink (the clock reading Home's own pixels, an opt-in accessibility service), Notification Center time groups, a Mute this channel action, what goes beside Home on the trifold and tablets, StandBy 2, Drop to Home and
the Icon Actions picker.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Folio's panels over other apps:** turn it on, open an app, use a one-hand operation shortcut or Routine, then Reply to a message.
   Whether the reply box opens over another app at all is the biggest unknown in this beta.
2. **Rows 6 and 7** on the cover and the unfolded screen: how small the icons get, and whether a screen scrolls.
3. **Notification sections:** what ends up in Quiet, and whether anything urgent is hidden there.
4. **Widgets make room:** drag and resize widgets over full pages, then Undo.
5. **The unfolded layouts of the shade:** Card and Side by side.
6. **The ... button and Clear per app,** and **Add to Home from Spotlight.**
7. **Backups and imports:** export a layout while a widget's app is disabled and import it again; cancel an install prompt; share a .foliopkg from a slow app.
8. **Animations:** all of the above at normal speed, with Animation Speed slower, and with Reduce Motion on.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Numbers

Rule B on the Fold8, 22 and 23 Oct 2026: Folio Dev debug builds compiled for speed, scripted swipes, `dumpsys gfxinfo`, the first run of
each set dropped as a warm-up, medians over the rest. Baseline is the beta 3 tag build (918dfbcb); this beta is the candidate at 5945913a.
The gate is at most +1.0 point janky and +1.0 ms p95. The runs are noisier than the gate, so read it as no regression, not as faster.

| Test | Beta 3 | This beta |
|---|---|---|
| Home swipe, cover | 17.9% janky, p95 38 ms | 17.8%, 40 ms |
| Home swipe, inner | 25.0%, 36 ms | 18.6%, 34 ms |
| App Library scroll, inner | 5.1%, 20 ms | 5.1%, 19 ms |

The same build moved between 13% and 25% janky from one run to the next on the cover, and a freshly installed build runs worse for its
first five or so runs. The cover Home swipe difference of 2 ms at p95 is inside that spread. Idle frames, startup, memory and the App
Library scroll on the cover were not measured.
