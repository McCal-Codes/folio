# Folio 0.6.8-beta.1 for supporters

The first beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth". It carries what is already on `main` and lands the
rest of 0.6.8 over later betas. Version `0.6.8-beta.1`, versionCode 6080, so it installs over 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta, the path 0.6.7-beta.3 used. The stable 0.6.8 comes after
at least a day on this beta and the rest of 0.6.8 (REL-15, REL-20).

## What's in it

From `CHANGELOG.md` › 0.6.8:
- **Widgets, one app at a time** (#178): a row of app icons over the widget picker, one row per app while browsing.
- **A shorter Settings list** (#180): 26 rows become 16; a General page with Language; one Support Folio row; the
  Market inside Tweaks; Themes inside Wallpaper & Appearance.
- Invisible: Compose redraws fewer icons and island cards when nothing changed (#181).

Everything from 0.6.7.1 to 0.6.7.3 is included too (search, Flipbook's style, the photo tile, backups, Settings
search for tweaks, two Wallpaper & Appearance rows).

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Settings on the unfolded screen.** The split view was not checked: does each moved page (Software Update,
   Help, Roadmap, Credits, Advanced, Supporter, Supporters, Market, Themes) highlight the right row in the sidebar,
   and does Back go where you expect?
2. **Settings › General › Language, then Back.** Coming back from Android's language screen, one Back closed Settings
   instead of returning to General on the Fold8. Tell us whether it does for you.
3. **The Market row inside Tweaks.** Not seen on a phone yet.
4. **The widget picker with a work profile.** Folio's own widgets are left out when Work is chosen; check the rest.
5. **Anything that feels slower or faster on Home.** The redraw change should be invisible; if icons or the island
   flicker or lag, that's the one to report.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.1` on its head.
3. Build and sign with the release keystore (`scripts/release-signed.sh`; building the tagged commit is fine since
   #177). The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta may be built undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`, then check that Market ›
   Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
