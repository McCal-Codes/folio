# Folio 0.7.0-beta.1 for supporters

DRAFT 7 Nov 2026, from the 0.7.0 changelog on `main`. McCal rewrites this before it goes anywhere (REL-25) and adds his own items. The SHA-256, the VirusTotal link and the supporter-only labels are not filled in.

The first beta of 0.7.0. Version `0.7.0-beta.1`, versionCode 7000, so it installs over 0.6.9 and every 0.6.9 beta, and the stable 0.7.0 will install over it. Supporters get it through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new

**StandBy**
- **Twelve clock looks:** Thin, Bold, Analog, Numerals, Flip, World, Solar, Words, Stack, Neon, Nixie and Minimal, each shown in its own style in the Settings preview.
- **Steady by default:** the colons, the Neon glow, the Nixie tubes and the outer-screen glow hold still. A "Keep the clock steady" switch in the StandBy 2 options lets them move again, in daytime only and never with Reduce Motion.
- **Cards:** tap or flick the card to cycle between Up Next, the Month Grid and Device Vitals. A soft edge glow in the app's color shines when a notification arrives, with an unread dot for each app.
- **Dual-screen ambient light:** the outer screen glows softly while the Fold is propped or folded in StandBy, matching your clock color.

**Panels and Home**
- **Panels follow your finger:** Notification Center and Control Center move up with your finger when you swipe them away, and close when you let go if you went far enough or flicked. A short pull springs back.
- **Pull tabs in full-screen apps:** in a video, a game or a reader that hides the status bar, two small tabs sit on the top edge: the left for Notification Center, the right for Control Center. A switch under "Pull down from the top of any app" turns them off.
- **Notification Center preview pane:** on screens 700 dp and wider, such as the unfolded Fold, a list on the left and the full message with quick actions on the right.
- **Island and status rail:** the island tints to the app's icon colors, with an optional rolling ticker, a flashlight indicator and a charging bolt beside the battery.
- **Home on a Fold:** closer two-page spacing when unfolded flat, folders that settle onto one screen half, page-edge resistance, and a blueprint preview when you drag or resize a widget.
- **Swipe to Discover** stays smooth with your Android wallpaper, without flashing the drawn background.

**Market and Settings**
- **Compatibility before Get:** the install sheet says up front whether a package works on your phone, works with a limit or cannot be installed, and why. Get is off, with the reason beside it, whenever the installer would refuse.
- **Phone settings in Spotlight:** find settings such as Display and Notifications from multiword queries. Search opens the matching setting.

The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is machine-translated and has not had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Pull tabs:** open a video or a game that hides the status bar, pull the left tab down for Notification Center and the right for Control Center. Turn the switch off in Settings and check the tabs go.
2. **Panels follow your finger:** open Notification Center, swipe up slowly and let go early (it should spring back), then swipe up and flick (it should close).
3. **StandBy clocks:** in Settings > Fold & Displays > StandBy 2, try each clock look. They should hold still. Turn off "Keep the clock steady" in daytime and check the colons move again.
4. **StandBy on the Fold:** prop the Fold in tent or half-open posture. Check the outer screen glow, the edge glow when a notification arrives, and tapping the card.
5. **Compatibility before Get:** open a package in the Market that needs a feature your phone lacks and one that works on only one screen. The sheet should say so before you tap Get.
6. **Phone settings in Spotlight:** pull up Spotlight and type "display" or "notifications" and open the match.
7. **Unfolded Fold:** open Notification Center unfolded and tap a notification on the left for its preview on the right. Check the page spacing when you unfold flat and when you fold part way.
8. **Swipe to Google Discover** with an Android wallpaper set: no drawn background should flash.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
