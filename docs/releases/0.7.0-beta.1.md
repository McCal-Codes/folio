# Folio 0.7.0-beta.1 for supporters

The first beta of 0.7.0. Version `0.7.0-beta.1`, versionCode 7000, so it installs over 0.6.9 and every 0.6.9 beta, and the stable 0.7.0 will install over it. Supporters get it through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new

- **Notification Center preview pane:** on screens 700 dp and wider (such as the unfolded Fold), Notification Center displays a two-pane list and detail view with full message preview and inline quick actions like iPad Mail.
- **StandBy clock styles:** Settings > Fold & Displays adds Split-Flap mechanical plates, Neon Glow, Analog dial and Solar Arc, alongside rolling digit transitions.
- **StandBy interactive widgets:** tap the card in StandBy to cycle between Up Next Calendar events, Weather & Ambient Outlook, and Device Vitals & Battery Details, with a vertical dot stack indicator.
- **StandBy notifications:** a soft edge glow shines in the app's color when a notification arrives, with unread dots for each app and count in words.
- **Dual-screen ambient glow:** in tent mode or half open, the outer screen illuminates with a soft breathing ambient bedside lamp matching your selected clock color or night crimson.
- **StandBy preview in Settings:** an interactive preview card shows your selected clock style, widgets, music card, and Day or Night mode without cutoff.
- **Homescreen swipe to Discover:** swiping to Google Discover stays smooth with your Android system wallpaper without flashing the drawn Dunes background.
- **Tweak options:** community tweaks in Settings have expanded options and live interactive previews.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Notification Center preview pane**: unfold your phone and pull down Notification Center. Tap a notification on the left to see its full preview, actions, and inline reply on the right.
2. **StandBy clock styles** (Settings > Fold & Displays > StandBy > Clock style): try Split-Flap, Neon Glow, Analog and Solar Arc; look at how the clock face responds to Day and Night mode.
3. **StandBy interactive widgets**: tap the right-hand card in StandBy to cycle through Calendar, Weather Outlook and Battery Details.
4. **StandBy notifications** (Settings > Fold & Displays > StandBy > Notifications): trigger an incoming notification to see the edge glow in the app's accent color and unread dots appear.
5. **Dual-screen ambient glow**: place your Fold in tent mode or half-open with StandBy enabled and confirm the outer screen displays the soft breathing glow.
6. **Swipe to Google Discover**: with an Android system wallpaper applied, swipe left into Discover and confirm no drawn background flashes through.
7. **StandBy preview card**: in Settings, switch through the preview pills (Widgets, Clock, Music) and Day/Night toggle to confirm sizing is crisp and well-proportioned.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
