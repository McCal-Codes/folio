# Folio 0.6.8: Your language, your Home, and Smooth

The stable 0.6.8. It is the app of `v0.6.8-beta.7` and nothing else: REL-4b, checked on this bump, fails if anything that
goes into the APK differs from that tag, apart from the version, the dated changelog and the roadmap. The full list of
what is new is the `## [0.6.8]` section of [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the beta notes are
[0.6.8-beta.7](0.6.8-beta.7.md) and the betas before it.

## Version

`folioVersion = "0.6.8"`, `versionCode` **6080**, the same as every 0.6.8 beta, so it installs over beta.7 and the betas
before it, and over 0.6.7.3 (6073). The changelog's `[0.6.8]` is dated (REL-10), and the roadmap marks every 0.6.8 item
Done (REL-14b checks that none is left Building or Planned). Faster Folio's line now says only what shipped: a quicker
start. Smoother swiping is its own 0.6.9 item.

## Before the bump

1. **Beta.7 is the last beta.** It is beta.6 plus four small fixes (#260 to #263), and McCal chose to ship it as the
   stable without a further day of supporter soak (REL-20 asks for that day only for installer changes, and beta.7 has
   none). Beta.6 itself had been out since 2 Oct 2026 with one report (#256, not a regression from these fixes).
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.7` on `main` at the merge of the beta.7 bump (3ba4e680) first, so REL-4b has something to compare to.
   `main` has had no app change since (the freeze row in `docs/in-flight.md`, removed by this bump).
3. Not yet checked by hand on the Fold8: Arrange Like iPhone, a Home change followed by leaving, TalkBack in Korean.
   The scripted smoke on the Fold8 cover passed 13 of 14; the one failure, "Home is up", is unexplained (the phone was
   shared with another session).

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8` on it.
3. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh` from the tag, clean tree.
4. Publish on McCal-Codes/folio as Latest. In the message: the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report (REL-23)
   with the usual heads-up about small engines, and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24); no new
   permission since 0.6.7.3. Release notes are in McCal's words (REL-25).
5. Check Settings › Software Update on a phone on 0.6.7.3 offers it, and that the website (`foliolauncher.com`) picks up
   the release.
