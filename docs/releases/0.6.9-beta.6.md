## What's new since beta.5

- **Control Center fills the window and its pages go down:** it spreads across the window in pairs of columns (4, 6 or 8) and does not
  scroll: what does not fit goes on another Controls page, and Media and Connectivity are pages of their own. A swipe down moves to the
  next page and a swipe up goes back. A swipe up on the first page pulls the whole panel up and closes it once you pass a short distance or
  flick; a shorter pull springs back. It also closes with the new close button beside power (where the header has room), a tap on the
  margins around the controls (a tap between controls does nothing), a tap outside, Back, and TalkBack's Close action.
- **Page buttons and battery beside the grid:** from a phone's width, the page buttons stand beside the grid with a battery ring above them
  (percent, green while charging). On a short window, such as a phone on its side, the pages become small dots in the header.
- **Control Center look:** Settings > Notifications > Panels > Control Center look has Clear glass (the start), Frosted and Solid. Clear
  glass is as light as the picture behind it allows; the dim and the tiles darken only as far as white text needs (4.5 to 1).
- **Control Center size:** Settings > Notifications > Control Center size now runs Small, Compact, Standard, Large and Extra Large. Every
  size is checked to fit without scrolling.
- **Tiles you can feel:** a Control Center tile dips a little under your finger and springs back, and a tall slider shows its percent while
  you drag it. With Reduce Motion a tile only dims.
- **Sliders follow the phone:** with Control Center open, the volume slider moves with the side keys and the system panel, and the
  brightness slider moves when brightness changes outside Folio. Nothing is watched while Control Center is closed.
- **Tap the status rail for Control Center:** on Home, a tap on the Side Bar's status opens Control Center. It is off while Home is being
  edited or an icon is dragged. Pressing the rail now shows on its own capsule, not on a second pill above it.
- **Pull down from any app (off to start with):** Settings > Notifications > Panels > Pull down from the top of any app, available once
  Folio's panels over other apps is on. A pull down on the left half of an app's top edge opens Notification Center and on the right half
  Control Center, in place of the phone's shade, and a diagonal pull from the corner counts. While it is on, a thin strip the height of the
  status bar takes the touches there; full screen apps and Home keep their own. It uses the gestures service you already have.
- **Blur behind the panels on any phone:** over another app, on a phone whose system will not blur windows (One UI turns it off), Folio's
  panels now sit on a blurred picture of that app. One screenshot is taken before the panel appears, shrunk at once and blurred once; it
  stays in memory, is never saved or sent, and is dropped when the panel closes. Blur at zero or Reduce Transparency turns it off. This
  uses a screenshot capability on the gestures service, so turn Folio's gestures service off and on once after updating.
- **Panels open smoother over Home, and open folders blur Home:** the blur behind Notification Center and Control Center is one picture of
  Home taken when the panel opens, instead of a live blur on every frame. An open folder now blurs Home behind it, fading in with the
  folder's dim and card, and the folder's name no longer lands on the clock and date.
- **Now Playing in Control Center:** previous, play and next are at least 48 dp tall, and the tile has a scrubber with times and a spoken
  description.
- **Settings in three groups:** General and Accessibility first, then Launcher (wallpaper, Home Screen, icons, Today View, Search & App
  Library, Gestures & Actions), then Extensions (Dynamic Island, notifications, Focus, Fold & Displays, Side Key, Lock Cover, Tweaks).
  Privacy & Permissions and Support Folio moved into General.
- **Apps at the top on a phone:** a new Folio on a phone-size screen starts with Home's apps at the top (Settings > Home Screen & Dock >
  Position > Apps). A layout you already have keeps what it has, and Reset this layout goes back to the top.
- **Pick your Home apps in setup:** first setup has a new Your Home apps page after the starting point. It shows Home's grid live as you
  tick apps, with up to eight everyday apps ticked, one for each job Folio finds on the phone (phone, messages, camera, browser, photos,
  maps, calendar, Settings, and so on), and every other app under Show all apps. Nothing is applied until Continue. When two apps share a
  name (two Calendars, say), each row shows its package name underneath. The first page also has Use Folio now, which puts the starter
  apps on Home, in place of the apps Folio first placed there, and goes straight to Make Folio Your Home.
- **Smart clock ink (optional, off to start with):** with Android's own wallpaper behind Home, Settings > Wallpaper & Appearance > Text on
  Home has a Smart clock ink switch. It turns on its own accessibility service, Folio Smart clock ink, in Android's Accessibility settings.
  With it on, the Big Clock takes a picture of Home only while Folio's Home is in front, measures the part under the clock, throws the
  picture away at once and picks light or dark text from what is really behind it. Only a few brightness numbers are kept; nothing is saved
  or sent.

## Fixes

- **Short windows keep every row of Home on screen:** a phone turned sideways (and a small split-screen window) no longer cuts off its last
  row of apps under the page controls. Home counts the space the page controls really take, splits a short window into two columns from
  588 dp wide, and shrinks icons a little further before it ever cuts a row.
- **No crash when you turn from landscape to portrait on the Home app:** an App Library card measured very narrow for a moment during the
  turn could be given a negative corner size, which closed Folio. Card sizes are now never below zero. (Seen and fixed on an emulator; not
  seen on a real rotation or fold.)
- **An app you turn off keeps its place on Home:** disabling an app (or putting it to sleep) and enabling it again used to leave a hole where
  its icon was on Home and in the Dock. It now comes back in its spot.
- **Display size and large text:** changing Display size in Android's settings no longer sends Settings or the Market back to their first
  page. At 200% font size the Market's tab names and Featured banner no longer cut off, Home's labels and the clock and date widgets follow
  the system text size up to 130%, and Control Center's header and strip icons grow with the font.
- **Widgets that came back:** a widget whose app was turned off and on again (the Maps traffic widget, for one) kept saying "Widget
  unavailable" until Folio restarted. Home now looks again, and a tile that still can't be found offers Choose again and Remove.
- **Home no longer turns back into the Welcome:** pressing Home while the Welcome was up used to bring it back at the page you left the next
  time the screen was rebuilt. It now counts as Skip for now; Settings > Help > Show Welcome Again resumes where you were.
- **Brightness and volume are always in Control Center:** with the Media and sound page off and a first page too full for the two sliders,
  they were not shown at all. They now move to the next Controls page.
- **The Rows menu names Automatic correctly:** with a fixed number of rows chosen (6, say), the Automatic option read "Automatic (6)" even
  though Automatic would give 4. It now always shows what Automatic would give.
- **Other small fixes from the 27 Oct bug hunts:** Folio no longer closes when a menu opens in a window narrower than about 290 dp; a
  damaged extra sent to Home by another app, or a damaged saved island position, can no longer crash it; the Music widget lets go of the
  last track when notification access is turned off; Safe Mode stops the screen pictures (Smart clock ink and the panel blur); Control
  Center closes the same way after a fold or a Display size change; and a layout backup keeps a Music widget.

## Supporters and Folio Dev try these first

These are built into this beta and closed for everyone else. Supporters with the beta scope and Folio Dev see them now. Their gates open
in the release named beside each one.

- **Control Center without repeats (opens in 0.6.9):** the Media and Connectivity pages show only when the first page cannot hold what they
  hold, so on a roomy screen the strip has no pages that repeat the first. While nothing is playing, Now Playing is one slim row with the
  title. The battery ring stands clear of the page dots.
- **More Control Center settings (opens in 0.6.9):** Settings > Notifications & Control Center has cards for Pages (switches for the Media
  and sound and Connectivity pages, and Open on Controls or the last page used), Sliders (haptic ticks while dragging), Controls (an Edit
  controls row, and Close after a toggle, off to start with) and Reset Control Center, which keeps your controls and offers Undo for a few
  seconds. The brightness slider gets an Auto chip.
- **Adjust the island (opens in 0.6.9):** Settings > Dynamic Island > Adjust island shows the island on a dimmed screen with a round handle
  to make it wider, arrows to nudge it into place, Fit to camera (when Android reports a wider camera than the lens the island wraps) and
  Reset. The width is saved per screen and orientation, and TalkBack gets Wider, Narrower and four Move actions.
- **More island style (opens in 0.7.0):** Settings > Dynamic Island gets an Island style card for power users: color, corner radius,
  animation speed, haptics and gesture mapping, each with a Reset.
- **Home spaced like the iPhone Duo (opens in 0.6.9):** where a window has free height, the rows spread toward the Duo's row spacing, the
  free height above and below Home is shared evenly, and the dock apps are a little larger (about 80% of Home's icons). A Rows number you
  picked is kept.
- **Hinge detents (opens in 0.7.0):** Settings > Fold & Displays has a Hinge detents card. As the Fold opens or closes you feel soft ticks
  at 45, 90 and 135 degrees and a firmer click when it lies flat. Light, Medium or Firm. With the optional root hinge angle all the stops
  play; without root Folio ticks once each time the Fold changes position. It replaces the single tick at halfway, follows the Haptic
  Feedback switch, and needs no new permission.
- **A Music widget (opens in 0.7.0):** in the widget gallery under Folio, in three shapes: a slim Strip, a Card and a Poster with a blurred
  backdrop and a wave. It reads the track from your player the way the island does. Play, pause, previous and next are 48 dp targets, times
  show only when the player gives a duration, and the wave freezes when paused or with Reduce Motion. With nothing playing it says Not
  Playing. It cannot be made narrower than 3 columns.

## Permissions

Beta 6 declares the same 16 permissions as beta 5 (the manifest's `uses-permission` lines are unchanged since `v0.6.9-beta.5`).
[PERMISSIONS.md](https://github.com/McCal-Codes/folio/blob/main/PERMISSIONS.md) explains each one. Two things changed in the accessibility
services, and they are the kind of thing REL-24 asks us to say:

- Folio's gestures service can now take one screenshot, only to blur the picture behind a panel over another app (see Blur behind the
  panels above). It still does not read window contents or tap for you. This is why the service has to be turned off and on once.
- Smart clock ink is a second accessibility service, off until you turn it on in Android Settings > Accessibility. It can take a picture of
  the screen and nothing else. It is not a new manifest permission.
