# Folio 0.6.8-beta.7 for supporters, the release candidate

The seventh beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth", and its release candidate, replacing beta.6. If
nothing in it breaks, the stable 0.6.8 ships this beta's app with only the version, the dated changelog and the
roadmap changed (REL-4b, checked on the stable's bump, and REL-13). Version `0.6.8-beta.7`, versionCode 6080 like the
betas before it, so it installs over beta.6 and 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable follows after at least a day on this one with
nothing broken.

## What's new since beta.6

Four small fixes taken from the 0.6.9 work, and nothing else.

- **Arrange Like iPhone no longer crashes Folio** (#262): it closed Folio as soon as it found an app for any iPhone
  spot. This was in 0.6.7.3 and beta.6 too.
- **A change right before you leave Home is kept** (#260): a change made in the last third of a second before leaving
  Folio or folding the phone could be lost. A file that isn't a Folio theme but is very large is refused instead of
  read whole, icon-pack lookups and the Diagnostics trail no longer wait on the thread that draws Home, and an update
  that can't be put in place says so.
- **Gestures say what they need, and Safe Mode pauses the Market refresh** (#261): Lock, Screenshot and a Do Not
  Disturb action with the service or access off now say what to turn on instead of doing nothing.
- **TalkBack follows your language** (#263): in Korean and Chinese, a Focus's switch, the reply field and Send on a
  message, Show all apps, Open, Moving, Choose and an install's progress were read in English. The reply Send button, a
  message's action buttons and search's Clear have a 48 dp tap area.

Everything in beta.6 is still here. The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is Claude's translation and has not
had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Settings › Home Screen & Dock › Arrange Like iPhone**: it should lay out Home's first page and dock, not close
   Folio. Undo it from Layout History.
2. **Change something on Home and leave straight away** (swipe to another app, or fold): it should still be there.
3. **Korean or Chinese with TalkBack on**: reply to a message, open the App Library's category "Show all", watch an
   install. Is it all in your language?
4. **A gesture set to Lock or Screenshot with the accessibility service off**: it should say what to turn on.
5. Then beta.6's list.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit. It keeps the in-flight row (renamed `release-068-beta7`): no app changes
   merge to `main` until the stable is out.
2. Run the full checks and the scripted smoke test on the Fold8 against the merge, then tag `v0.6.8-beta.7` on it.
3. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh`. The changelog stays `Unreleased`.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since beta.6.
5. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
6. After a day with nothing broken, the stable: this beta's app with the version set to `0.6.8`, the changelog dated
   and the roadmap's 0.6.8 items marked done.
