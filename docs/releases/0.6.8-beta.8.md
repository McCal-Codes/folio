# Folio 0.6.8-beta.8 for supporters, the release candidate

The eighth beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth", and its release candidate, replacing beta.7. If
nothing in it breaks, the stable 0.6.8 ships this beta's app with only the version, the dated changelog and the
roadmap changed (REL-4b, checked on the stable's bump, and REL-13). Version `0.6.8-beta.8`, versionCode 6080 like the
betas before it, so it installs over beta.7, beta.6 and 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable follows after at least a day on this one with
nothing broken.

## What's new since beta.7

Three small fixes from a code review of beta.7, and nothing else (#270).

- **An icon pack Folio can't read doesn't slow the App Library:** a pack with no usable list of icons was read again
  for every icon. It is left alone for half a minute after it fails, then tried again.
- **Apply and Undo for a theme go in order:** choosing a theme and then Undo within a moment of each other could end
  with the theme applied instead of undone.
- **TalkBack reads the Focus switch once:** in Control Center it read "Turn off Focus, On, Switch". It reads the
  Focus's name and whether it is on.

Beta.7 added four fixes to beta.6 (#260 to #263): Arrange Like iPhone no longer crashes Folio, a change right before you
leave Home is kept, gestures say what they need and Safe Mode pauses the Market refresh, and TalkBack follows your
language. Everything in them is still here. The Korean and Chinese text of the newer lines is Claude's translation and
has not had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Settings › Home Screen & Dock › Arrange Like iPhone**: it should lay out Home's first page and dock, not close
   Folio. Undo it from Layout History.
2. **Change something on Home and leave straight away** (swipe to another app, or fold): it should still be there.
3. **Apply a theme, then Undo straight away**: the look before it should come back.
4. **Control Center with TalkBack on**: the Focus switch should read the mode's name, then on or off.
5. **Korean or Chinese with TalkBack on**: reply to a message, open the App Library's "Show all", watch an install.
6. Then beta.6's list.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit. It keeps the in-flight row (renamed `release-068-beta7`): no app changes
   merge to `main` until the stable is out.
2. Run the full checks and the scripted smoke test on the Fold8 against the merge, then tag `v0.6.8-beta.8` on it.
3. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh`. The changelog stays `Unreleased`.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since beta.6.
5. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
6. After a day with nothing broken, the stable: this beta's app with the version set to `0.6.8`, the changelog dated
   and the roadmap's 0.6.8 items marked done.
