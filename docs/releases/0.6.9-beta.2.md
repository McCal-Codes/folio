# Folio 0.6.9-beta.2 for supporters

DRAFT, 9 Oct 2026. Claude's wording from the changelog; McCal rewrites it before it goes anywhere (REL-25). Lines in
angle brackets wait on what actually merges: the beta 2 contents are not final yet.

The second beta of 0.6.9, the new animations. Version `0.6.9-beta.2`, versionCode 6090 like beta.1: a beta shares its
release's code, and Software Update orders betas by name, so beta.2 installs over beta.1 and over 0.6.8 (6080).
Supporters get it the way they got beta.1, through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.1

- <the new animations, if they go in: sheets, menus and alerts on shared springs, a press and lift on icons, page dots
  that stretch, behind the New motion switch for supporters first (#321 to #323, once their before and after numbers are in)>
- **Animation Speed reaches more motion:** panels, the Dynamic Island, the notification shade, switches, segmented
  controls and the icon fan follow Settings > Animation Speed, on a handful of shared springs.
- **Notices that fit, with Undo:** notices in the island size themselves to their text and stay clear of the camera and
  the fold, and when Folio updates a package by itself the notice says so with an Undo.
- **Setup progress and two first-use hints:** Finish setting up counts the steps done, a Focus that turns itself on says
  why, and Folio tells you once how to edit Home and that the open screen is the same Home with more room.
- **Safe Mode that recovers:** an install cut short is put back on the next start, and native crashes and freezes at
  the start count toward Safe Mode, while Android closing Folio for memory does not.
- **Performance log by what Folio was doing:** the fold animation, Home swipes, folders and widget resizing get their
  own frame timing in the report.
- Fixes: Email a Report opens your mail app (or the share sheet when there is none), turning System Bridge access back
  on restarts the root feed at once, and "Separate sizes when upright" says it gives the screen its own sizes, not its
  own layout (#296).

## What to try, most likely broken first

What to Test on the beta lists these (#319):

1. **Notices:** on the cover, the open screen and half folded; long text, the camera cutout, the fold.
2. **Undo from the update notice:** let a package update itself, then Undo; Home should not change.
3. **An install cut short:** the next start should say which package did not finish and put it back.
4. **Animation Speed:** try 0, 0.5 and 1 and open panels, the island, the shade and a folder.
5. **The hints and Finish setting up:** each hint shows once; the step count and ticks stay where they were.
6. <the new animations, with the New motion switch on and off>

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
