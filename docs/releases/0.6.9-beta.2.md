# Folio 0.6.9-beta.2 for supporters

DRAFT, 10 Oct 2026. Claude's wording from the changelog; McCal rewrites it before it goes anywhere (REL-25).

The second beta of 0.6.9, the new motion. Version `0.6.9-beta.2`, versionCode 6090 like beta.1: a beta shares its
release's code, and Software Update orders betas by name, so beta.2 installs over beta.1 and over 0.6.8 (6080).
Supporters get it the way they got beta.1, through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.1

- **New motion** (Settings > General > Advanced > New motion, supporters first): icons dip the same way under a finger
  and lift with a soft shadow while their menu is open, and after a tap an icon stays dipped until the app is up. The
  active page dot stretches toward the next page as you swipe. Pages, sheets, menus and alerts settle on shared springs,
  the Market's tab highlight glides, badge counts roll, and a finished install pops a check. Off, or with Reduce Motion,
  everything moves as it did.
- **Animation Speed reaches more motion:** panels, the Dynamic Island, the notification shade, switches, sheets, alerts
  and the icon fan follow Settings > Animation Speed.
- **Notices that fit, with Undo:** notices in the island size themselves to their text and stay clear of the camera and
  the fold, and when Folio updates a package by itself the notice says so with an Undo.
- **Setup progress and two first-use hints:** Finish setting up counts the steps done, a Focus that turns itself on says
  why, and Folio tells you once how to edit Home and that the open screen is the same Home with more room.
- **Safe Mode that recovers:** an install cut short is put back on the next start, and native crashes and freezes at the
  start count toward Safe Mode, while Android closing Folio for memory does not.
- **The Market lists bigger apps:** up to 100 MB for an app from a source (packages stay at 20 MB).
- **Performance log by what Folio was doing:** the fold animation, Home swipes, folders and widget resizing get their own
  frame timing in the report.
- Fixes:
  - A Focus set to turn on while charging now does, also at a Samsung charge limit, and a Focus you chose stays on when
    another one's trigger fires (#328).
  - Full-Width Home is centered, and its App Library has a margin on both sides.
  - Bottom of Home says where Search goes (#325), and the Search button no longer squeezes the dock (#256).
  - One prompt at a time on a fresh start, and the Home preview in Settings shows your real battery and signal.
  - Email a Report opens your mail app, System Bridge changes take effect at once, and "Separate sizes when upright"
    says what it does (#296).

The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is Claude's translation and has not had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **New motion** on and off (Settings > General > Advanced): press and hold an icon, tap an app that is slow to open,
   swipe pages and watch the dots, open a sheet and a menu, switch Market tabs. With Reduce Motion on, nothing should slide.
2. **A charging Focus:** set a Focus to turn on when charging, plug in, unplug; try it at a charge limit.
3. **Full-Width Home** (Settings > Home Screen & Dock > Layout): the apps and the App Library sit centered.
4. **Notices:** on the cover, the open screen and half folded; long text, the camera cutout, the fold.
5. **Undo from the update notice:** let a package update itself, then Undo; Home should not change.
6. **An install cut short:** the next start should say which package did not finish and put it back.
7. **Animation Speed:** try 0, 0.5 and 1 and open panels, the island, the shade and a folder.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
