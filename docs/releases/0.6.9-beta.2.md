# Folio 0.6.9-beta.2 for supporters

Published 12 Oct 2026 as a pre-release on McCal-Codes/folio-beta (supporters only), built and signed from the tag
`v0.6.9-beta.2` on c614e10. The text below is McCal's, as published.

**Folio 0.6.9 beta 2, for supporters**

Hey everyone, beta 2 is here! This one is all about motion. I spent a lot of time making Folio feel smoother and more alive, and I'm really happy with how it turned out, so I can't wait for you to try it. Thanks again for all the testing and reports on beta 1, they helped a ton.

It installs over beta 1 and 0.6.8, and it's signed with the same key as every earlier release. Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta.

**What's new since beta 1**

- **New motion** (Settings › General › Advanced › New motion, supporters first): icons dip under your finger and lift with a soft shadow while their menu is open, and after a tap an icon stays dipped until the app is up. The page dot stretches toward the next page as you swipe. Pages, sheets, menus and alerts settle on shared springs, the Market's tab highlight glides, badge counts roll, and a finished install pops a check. Turn it off to compare with how things moved before.
- **Animation Speed reaches more of Folio:** panels, the Dynamic Island, the notification shade, switches, sheets, alerts and the icon fan all follow Settings › Animation Speed now.
- **Notices that fit, with Undo:** notices in the island size themselves to their text and stay clear of the camera and the fold, and when Folio updates a package by itself the notice says so, with an Undo.
- **Setup progress and two first-use hints:** Finish Setting Up counts the steps you've done, a Focus that turns itself on says why, and Folio tells you once how to edit Home and that the open screen is the same Home with more room.
- **Safe Mode that recovers:** an install cut short is put back on the next start, and crashes and freezes right at the start count toward Safe Mode, while Android closing Folio for memory doesn't.
- **The Market lists bigger apps:** up to 100 MB for an app from a source (packages stay at 20 MB).
- **Performance log by what Folio was doing:** the fold animation, Home swipes, folders and widget resizing get their own frame timing in the report.
- **Fixes:**
  - A Focus set to turn on while charging now does, also at a Samsung charge limit, and a Focus you chose stays on when another one's trigger fires (#328).
  - Full-Width Home is centered, and its App Library has a margin on both sides.
  - Bottom of Home says where Search goes (#325), and the Search button no longer squeezes the dock (#256).
  - One prompt at a time on a fresh start, and the Home preview in Settings shows your real battery and signal.
  - Email a Report opens your mail app, System Bridge changes take effect right away, and "Separate sizes when upright" says what it does (#296).

The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is machine-translated and hasn't had a native review yet.

Everything in beta 1 is still here.

**What to test** (most likely to be broken first)

1. **New motion**, on and off (Settings › General › Advanced): press and hold an icon, tap an app that's slow to open, swipe pages and watch the dots, open a sheet and a menu, switch Market tabs. With Reduce Motion on, nothing should slide.
2. **A charging Focus:** set a Focus to turn on when charging, plug in, unplug, and try it at a charge limit.
3. **Full-Width Home** (Settings › Home Screen & Dock › Layout): the apps and the App Library should sit centered.
4. **Notices:** on the cover screen, the open screen and half folded; long text, the camera cutout, the fold.
5. **Undo from the update notice:** let a package update itself, then Undo. Home shouldn't change.
6. **An install cut short:** the next start should say which package didn't finish and put it back.
7. **Animation Speed:** try 0, 0.5 and 1, then open panels, the island, the shade and a folder.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

**Known issue:** Back doesn't close an open folder (it's been like that since beta 1). Tap outside the folder to close it for now. It's already fixed for beta 3.
