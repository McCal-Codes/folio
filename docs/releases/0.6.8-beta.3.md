# Folio 0.6.8-beta.3 for supporters

The third beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth". Version `0.6.8-beta.3`, versionCode 6080 like
beta.1 and beta.2, so it installs over beta.2 and over 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable 0.6.8 comes after at least a day on a beta and
the rest of 0.6.8 (REL-15, REL-20). This beta carries the Market's updates for apps it lists (#187), which change how
Folio installs over an app, so it needs that day.

## What's new since beta.2

- **StandBy without the fold** (#206, #207, #211, #212): StandBy comes on while the phone charges on its side,
  in any pose, as on iPhone, and optionally when it stands as a tent. Choose Folio StandBy as Android's screen saver
  under Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy › Screen Saver, and it carries on over the lock screen while the phone
  locks underneath; pick the phone up or swipe up from the bottom to leave. On a charger StandBy no longer keeps the
  screen on, so the phone always locks. Plugged in counts as charging at a charge limit, and a phone without a fold
  is offered only the ways it can use.
- **Home your way** (#210): up to six apps in the dock (Settings › Home Screen & Dock › Layout › Apps in the dock),
  Full-Width Home for phones, which puts the dock along the bottom and drops the status Side Bar, and a separate
  layout for the unfolded screen held upright. Each stays as it was until you turn it on.
- **Duet** (#205): the fold animation is a tweak with six looks (iPhone Duo, Duo, Classic Glass, Deep, Subtle and
  Minimal) on its own page under Settings › Tweaks.
- **Flipbook** (#191, #192, #197, #201): its effects are on Flipbook's own page, Inside Cube and Stack join Cube and
  Carousel, and an effect in the Market says it needs Flipbook before you get it.
- **Faster after a start** (#194, #195): Home is ready in about a sixth of the time, the App Library in under half.
- **The Market** (#187, #190, #193, #196, #198): updates for apps a source lists, like Keyd; a package file opens
  its install sheet again; packages Folio can't use yet are refused instead of installing as nothing.
- **Folders, search and icons** (#209): add several apps to a folder at once, more search engines and your own, an
  icon style for one app, and Home beside an app in split screen.
- **Switches** (#208): Folio or Android style at setup, Folio's notification shade from other apps, how long Touch
  and Hold takes, Add Folio Settings to Home, and a switch for Today View's Suggestions.
- **Fixes** (#202, #212 and others): hidden apps stay hidden, Settings keeps your place on Back, tapping a clock or
  a date opens its app, TalkBack says what controls do, and the charging island shows when you plug in at a charge
  limit.

Everything in beta.2 is still here.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **StandBy on the lock screen.** Set it up under Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy › Screen Saver, then leave
   the phone on a charger until the screen turns off. Does Folio StandBy come up over the lock screen? Does picking
   the phone up, or swiping up from the bottom, land on the lock screen rather than an unlocked Home? What happens
   if you unplug it while it's up, or leave it up all night?
2. **StandBy while charging on its side**, closed and open: it should come up about 5 seconds after you set the
   phone down, and the screen should still turn off at your normal timeout.
3. **App updates in the Market**, with Keyd: Update should show both versions and install over the old copy.
4. **Duet's six looks**, opening and closing, and its sliders.
5. **Six apps in the dock**, on both screens, and Full-Width Home on the cover.
6. **Folders**: Add Apps with several picked, then Undo.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.3` on its head.
3. Build and sign with the release keystore (`scripts/release-signed.sh`). The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta
   may be built undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`, then check that Market ›
   Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
