# Folio 0.6.7.2: three small fixes from 0.6.7's review

Three fixes on top of 0.6.7.1, all found in the review of #148 (real art) or while testing 0.6.7.1.

## What it fixes

- **Flipbook remembers your style** (#170). Turning Flipbook off and on in Tweaks always brought back Cube, so a
  Carousel choice was lost. `lastPageEffect` is saved with the rest of the state; a 0.6.7 save reads it from the
  effect in use.
- **Your photo comes back in one tap** (#171). With an artwork behind Home, tapping Your Photo opened the picker.
  It now switches back to the saved photo; Choose a Different Photo still picks a new one.
- **Restored backups tidy up wallpapers** (#172). Restoring a Market backup left wallpaper packages it didn't carry
  on disk and in the Installed grid. The restore now prunes art the way Remove and Undo do.
