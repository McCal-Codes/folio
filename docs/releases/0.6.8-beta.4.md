# Folio 0.6.8-beta.4 for supporters

The fourth beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth". Version `0.6.8-beta.4`, versionCode 6080 like
the betas before it, so it installs over 0.6.7.3 (6073). `v0.6.8-beta.3` was tagged but no build was ever published
under it (there is no 0.6.8 release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta`), so the number moved on (REL-16) and this beta
carries everything beta.3 would have.

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable 0.6.8 comes after at least a day on a beta and
the rest of 0.6.8 (REL-15, REL-20). This beta carries the Market's updates for apps it lists (#187), which change how
Folio installs over an app, so it needs that day.

## What's new since 0.6.7.3

- **StandBy without the fold** (#206, #207, #211, #212, #216): StandBy comes on while the phone charges on its side,
  in any pose, as on iPhone, and optionally when it stands as a tent. Choose Folio StandBy as Android's screen saver
  under Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy › Screen Saver, and it carries on over the lock screen while the phone
  locks underneath; pick the phone up or swipe up from the bottom to leave. On a charger StandBy no longer keeps the
  screen on, so the phone always locks. Plugged in counts as charging at a charge limit, a phone without a fold is
  offered only the ways it can use, and on the cover Now Playing keeps its controls under the title.
- **Home your way** (#210, #213): the dock grows as you drag apps into it, up to six, with no setting to
  choose (drag an app onto a full dock and an open place appears; the bar and icons ease into their new spacing, and
  a dock already holding six shakes and ticks as it turns the app away). Full-Width Home for phones puts the dock
  along the bottom and drops the status Side Bar, and turning it off puts back what it replaced. There is also a
  separate layout for the unfolded screen held upright. Each stays as it was until you use it.
- **Settings in iOS order** (#215): General and Accessibility come first, Backup is inside General, Big Buttons has
  an Accessibility page of its own, and Settings search finds individual settings (Badge Size, Touch and Hold, Save
  Backup) and scrolls to the row and lights it.
- **Duet** (#205): the fold animation is a tweak with six looks (iPhone Duo, Duo, Classic Glass, Deep, Subtle and
  Minimal) on its own page under Settings › Tweaks.
- **Flipbook** (#191, #192, #197, #201): its effects are on Flipbook's own page, Inside Cube and Stack join Cube and
  Carousel, and an effect in the Market says it needs Flipbook before you get it.
- **Faster after a start** (#194, #195): Home is ready in about a sixth of the time, the App Library in under half.
- **The Market** (#187, #190, #193, #196, #198): updates for apps a source lists, like Keyd; a package file opens
  its install sheet again; packages Folio can't use yet are refused instead of installing as nothing.
- **Folders, search and icons** (#209): add several apps to a folder at once, more search engines and your own, an
  icon style for one app, and Home beside an app in split screen.
- **Switches** (#208): Folio or Android style at setup, Folio's notification shade from other apps, how long Touch
  and Hold takes, Add Folio Settings to Home, and a switch for Today View's Suggestions.
- **Fixes** (#202, #212 and others): hidden apps stay hidden, Settings keeps your place on Back, tapping a clock or
  a date opens its app, TalkBack says what controls do, and the charging island shows when you plug in at a charge
  limit.

Everything in 0.6.7.3 is still here.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **StandBy on the lock screen.** Set it up under Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy › Screen Saver, then leave
   the phone on a charger until the screen turns off. Does Folio StandBy come up over the lock screen? Does picking
   the phone up, or swiping up from the bottom, land on the lock screen rather than an unlocked Home? What happens
   if you unplug it while it's up, or leave it up all night?
2. **StandBy while charging on its side**, closed and open: it should come up about 5 seconds after you set the
   phone down, and the screen should still turn off at your normal timeout.
3. **App updates in the Market**, with Keyd: Update should show both versions and install over the old copy.
4. **Duet's six looks**, opening and closing, and its sliders.
5. **The dock growing**, on both screens: drag an app onto a full dock, drop it in the open place, then drag
   apps in until it holds six and try a seventh. Also Full-Width Home on the cover, and turning it off again.
6. **Settings search**: look for a setting by name ("badge size", "save backup") and tap the result. Does the page
   scroll to the row and light it? Are General and Accessibility at the top, where you expect them?
7. **Folders**: Add Apps with several picked, then Undo.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.4` on its head.
3. Build and sign with the release keystore: `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh` reads the two passwords from the
   macOS Keychain and runs `scripts/release-signed.sh` (which does the same on its own when the four
   `FOLIO_RELEASE_*` variables are set). The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta may be built undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since 0.6.7.3; it adds the
   shade shortcut entries and the StandBy screen saver, which PERMISSIONS.md covers.
5. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
