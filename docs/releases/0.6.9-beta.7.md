# Folio 0.6.9-beta.7 for supporters

DRAFT, 31 Oct 2026. The opening words are a stand-in and are McCal's to rewrite; the lists are a first pass from the changelog and the merged pull requests (REL-25).
Checked before this beta: the optimized build (the same kind the release uses) starts on a Fold emulator with no crash, Home, Today View as the page left of Home, Settings, Control Center and the wallpaper preview work there, and on a Fold8 the cover screen installs, starts and shows Home, Today View and the Crackgate joke over another app. Not checked on a phone yet: the inner screen, TalkBack, Reduce Motion, a fresh install and a real screen recording.

This is the build that becomes 0.6.9, with the early features open to everyone. What I need most is what is broken, so the list at the end says where to look first. Thank you for testing.

Version `0.6.9-beta.7`, versionCode 6090 like the earlier betas: a beta shares its release's code, and Software Update orders
betas by name, so beta.7 installs over beta.6 and the betas before it. Supporters get it through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.6

- **The early features are open:** the 0.6.9 features supporters could try first (Focus triggers, Dock grabbers, Market auto-updates, folder editing, Big Clock customizing, widgets that fill two rows, Adjust island, the Control Center reorder and panel switch, Crackgate and the wallpaper preview among them) are now on for everyone, with no early access switch needed. A supporter who turned New motion off in a beta stays on the new motion now that the switch is gone.
- **Home spaced like the iPhone Duo, and the new motion, for everyone:** on a phone-sized screen the rows spread toward the Duo's row spacing where there is free height and the dock apps are a little larger. Icons dip and lift under your finger, badges pop and roll, pages stretch and catch, the switch thumb widens, and the Market's tab highlight glides. All of it follows Reduce Motion and Animation Speed.
- **Switch between the panels:** two icons at the top of Notification Center and Control Center, a bell and sliders, move between them.
- **Folio tiles in Quick Settings:** add Folio Notifications and Folio Control Center to the phone's Quick Settings, and Notification Center has a Quick Settings button.
- **Choose which half of the top edge is Folio's:** Settings > Notifications & Control Center > Swipe down opens (Folio's panels, Folio notifications or Folio Control Center). A quick pull from the top edge counts even when Android cancels it.
- **Move controls by dragging:** in Control Center's edit mode, hold a control and drag it where you want it. A swipe up in the margins no longer closes Control Center, and a swipe up under the controls goes back a page.
- **Six more wallpapers and a full-screen preview:** Settings > Wallpaper & Appearance has six abstract photographs from Unsplash (each shows the title, the photographer and "Unsplash License") beside the two Hiroshige prints. Tapping one opens it full screen on a drawn Home with Cancel and Set; only Set changes anything.
- **Swiping right always lands somewhere:** when the Google app refuses to serve Discover to Folio (it only serves system apps and debug builds), Home shows Today View in that spot instead of an error page, and Settings > Today View says why. Folio asks again at every launch.
- **Crackgate, a joke:** Settings > Fold & Displays > Duet has a Crackgate joke switch, off to start with. With it on, Show me draws a pretend Duo hinge fault over other apps (never over Folio itself), clears when you shake the phone, and respects Reduce Motion. It needs Folio's gestures turned on. Nothing flashes and nothing makes a sound.
- **The island's color shows on the cover** when it opens, and Control Center's size setting says it only shows on wider screens.
- **Setup carries on after Folio becomes the Home app,** and an open screen held upright with its own layout uses the rows that fit it.
- **A quick swipe down from the top of an app opens the panel:** a fast pull used to be taken over by Android before Folio saw it move, and nothing opened. It counts now.

## Fixes

- **Settings search on the open screen:** with a long section name beside it ("Notifications & Control Center"), a result's title was squeezed to one letter per line in the narrow list. The title and the section now share the row.
- **Crackgate's Show me says when it needs gestures** instead of doing nothing, and What's New shows the StandBy tent title whole.
- **Storage names an app by its own label.**

## Still for supporters and Folio Dev

The 0.7.0 features stay closed to everyone else: the AirPods battery (with and without root), Private While Recording, Hinge detents, the Music widget, the Advanced island and Storage. Supporters with the beta scope still see them; the Control Center and wallpaper items above are open to everyone now.

## Permissions

Beta 7 declares 18 permissions, the same as beta 6 ([PERMISSIONS.md](https://github.com/McCal-Codes/folio/blob/main/PERMISSIONS.md) explains each one). The only manifest change is two Quick Settings tiles, which are not permissions and which Android only shows once you add them.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Panels over other apps:** the two Quick Settings tiles, the switch between Notification Center and Control Center, and Swipe down opens. Cover and inner, with the gestures service turned off and on once after updating.
2. **Wallpaper preview:** open a tile, Cancel, then Set; check the credit line, and that the picture shows behind Home.
3. **Swipe right from Home:** Today View appears, and Discover returns by itself if the Google app ever allows it.
4. **Control Center edit mode:** drag a control, add one back, undo.
5. **Crackgate (a joke):** turn it on, press Show me, leave Folio for another app, shake the phone. Please tell me if anything flickers.
6. **Settings search on the open screen,** for long names.
7. **New motion** for anyone who turned it off in an earlier beta: it is on now.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
