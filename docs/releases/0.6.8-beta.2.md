# Folio 0.6.8-beta.2 for supporters

The second beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth". Version `0.6.8-beta.2`, versionCode 6080 like
beta.1: a beta shares its release's code, and Software Update orders betas by name, so beta.2 installs over beta.1
and over 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it the way they got beta.1, through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable 0.6.8 comes after at
least a day on a beta and the rest of 0.6.8 (REL-15, REL-20).

## What's new since beta.1

- **Flipbook turns the way it was designed to** (#186): Cube and Carousel drew with their camera about six times
  too close, so a page shrank to a sliver partway through a swipe. The camera sits where it was meant to now, on
  every screen density. This is the one change people will see.
- **Page effects from the Market** (#183, #184, #185): a Market package can carry a page effect, and installed ones
  are listed under Settings › Gestures › Page Effects beside Cube and Carousel. Nothing to install yet: the first
  three (Tilt, Stack and Cylinder) wait in McCal-Codes/folio-tweaks#4 until this beta is out.

Everything in beta.1 is still here (the widget picker, the shorter Settings list, Compose stability, and 0.6.7.1 to
0.6.7.3).

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Flipbook, both styles, on both screens.** Cube should read as the sides of a box turning, Carousel as cards
   stepping back. Does either still look squashed, stutter at 120 Hz, or show a page's back?
2. **Swiping while dragging an app or resizing a widget.** Pages should slide flat, as before.
3. **Reduce Motion.** Every page effect should switch off.
4. From beta.1, still open: Settings on the unfolded screen (does each moved page highlight the right sidebar row?),
   and Back after Settings › General › Language.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.2` on its head.
3. Build and sign with the release keystore (`scripts/release-signed.sh`). The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta
   may be built undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`, then check that Market ›
   Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
5. Then folio-tweaks#4 can merge, once a phone on 0.6.7.3 shows that a package kind it doesn't know is skipped
   rather than breaking the source.
