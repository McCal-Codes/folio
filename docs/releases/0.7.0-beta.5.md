# Folio 0.7.0 beta 5

*Pre-release on folio-beta. Checked against the changelog and the manifest before it went out.*

Beta 5 is about choosing a wallpaper the way iOS does, finding and placing widgets, a calmer Market, and a Home that works on a flip's cover screen. Supporters on a monthly Ko-fi membership can also let Folio renew their code.

## What's new

- **Frame a wallpaper photo.** Pick a photo and it opens full screen over a drawn Home. Pinch to zoom and drag to move it, then Set keeps exactly what you framed. Cancel leaves your wallpaper as it was. A Blur switch softens the picture with a short fade, so icons and labels stand out on a busy photo.
- **Wallpaper from the Edit menu, or from Share.** While icons wiggle, Edit has a Wallpaper Photo row that opens your photos straight away, and Set or Cancel brings you back to Home. In Photos, Files or a browser, Share a picture to Folio Wallpaper and it opens in the preview, ready for one tap on Set. Nothing changes until you tap Set.
- **A new wallpaper fades in.** The old picture eases into the new one instead of switching at once. Reduce Motion keeps the instant switch.
- **Customize on the Edit menu.** A short sheet over Home shows the icon looks (Default, Dark, Clear and Tinted) on one of your own apps, and Home behind it changes as you tap.
- **Clear icons on a light Home.** The Clear look now draws a dark symbol on a darker tile over a light background, so the icons no longer almost disappear.
- **Removed icons shrink away.** Tapping the minus on an app or dock icon shrinks and fades it where it stood. Undo brings it back. Reduce Motion fades without the shrink.
- **Remove a folder from Home.** A folder gets the same gray minus as an app. Its apps stay installed and in the App Library, and Undo brings the folder back with everything in it. A folder's name also gets the soft label shadow an app has.
- **Pick a size and a spot for a widget.** Tapping a widget in the picker opens it large, with its description, a size switch and a map of the Home page showing what is taken. Drag it on the map to move it, drag its corner to resize it, then Add, or Place on Home screen to choose on Home itself. The widget is drawn live at the size it will have, and the picker has no grey frame, with a plus in the corner.
- **Search widget.** A Search box for Home, from the widget picker. Two rows tall it adds shortcuts to Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Display. Tapping it opens Spotlight.
- **More phone settings in Search.** Spotlight and the Search pill find eleven more of the phone's settings pages, such as Hotspot, VPN, Night Light, Battery Saver and Privacy, and the words people use for options inside the pages already there.
- **Is StandBy ready?** Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy names what each StandBy way that is switched on is waiting for, live, and stores or sends nothing.
- **Renew my monthly code automatically.** Settings › Supporter has a switch, off until you turn it on, for Ko-fi monthly members. When your code is in its last week or has ended, Folio picks up the code your newest payment earned, so you no longer open the email each month. It sends only the code you already have and checks the new one on your phone like any other. It shows only in the release build, and not on Folio Dev.
- **A calmer, Sileo-like Market.** A flat tab bar, plain rows, a small green check or blue arrow on packages you have, a tap on the current tab to go to the top, and a slim Get bar on long package pages. A package page opens on a color band with Version, Source and Works on in a row, and a line under the picture says what is new. Tweak pages open on a still picture, and Folio's own packages have shaded icons.
- **Apps in the Market.** A new Apps section points to apps of their own on Google Play, F-Droid or Obtainium, starting with Performatively. Folio does not install them or share anything with them. Performatively also has a banner on Featured.
- **Duet is a package.** The fold animation has its own page in the Folio source, like Cabinet, Harborline and the others. Duet itself works as before.
- **Duet has a new Duo Clip look.** It is the new default style, drawn from frame-by-frame measurements of the real fold: the half that folds goes soft over the first few degrees, then darkens from its outer edge toward the hinge, while the open half stays sharp and the whole screen no longer dims. The cover screen has the same look as it lights. Anyone who picked another Duet style keeps it. Nothing flashes.
- **Folding remembers where you were.** Each screen keeps its own Home page, so the cover shows the page it had after a fold and the inner screen goes back to its own on unfold. An open App Library stays open through a fold, with the same list, open category and scroll. Nothing moves while you are carrying an icon or a widget.
- **Badges show how many are unread.** An icon's number is the unread count the app reports, or the messages in its chat notifications, instead of how many notifications there are. Where an app gives no count Folio can trust, the badge is a dot rather than a guess.
- **Control Center drags like iOS.** A held control lifts and follows your finger, the place it would take is outlined, and a neighbor slides into the spot you leave. There is an Undo button, you can press and hold an empty spot to start editing, and TalkBack says each control's row, column and size. The editor splits controls into pages instead of scrolling. With Reduce Motion nothing slides except the control in your finger.
- **Control Center connectivity, as on the iPhone Duo.** Holding the Wi-Fi block opens two columns of glass tiles on a solid surface, about 60 dp shorter, so the last row is no longer cut off on a flip's cover screen.
- **Roadmap.** Two ideas under Exploring: a Wear OS watch, and a Home Assistant page in Control Center. Nothing is built for either.

## Fixed

- **Home on a flip's cover screen.** Icons keep at least 40 dp and the dock's 32 dp instead of shrinking to fit four rows, Edit's buttons wrap onto a second row, a page that scrolls fades at its edge, and the dock on Today View no longer runs under the arrow and Search buttons. Settings has a one-line header there, and Spotlight keeps its first result in view with the keyboard up.
- **Focus on Samsung phones with Android 15.** Turning a Focus on, or a schedule or charging doing it, could close Folio. Folio now falls back to the older Do Not Disturb rule, and a failure there can no longer take Home down.
- **Control Center connectivity list.** The names of the controls under it no longer run through the open list.
- **Widget picker details.** The page map names each spot for TalkBack and announces when the fit changes, and the preview keeps its size at larger text.

## Please try first

Set a wallpaper from a photo: pinch and drag it, try Blur, then Set. Then share a picture to Folio from Photos. After that, open the widget picker, pick a size and drag a widget to a spot on the map. Fold and unfold the phone slowly with Duet on its new Duo Clip look, and tell me if anything pops, jumps or flashes. If you have a flip, check Home and Settings on the cover screen. If you are a monthly Ko-fi member, turn on Renew my monthly code automatically and tell me when it picks up a code.

The Search widget, the Control Center editor and the Photos widget are early-access features: they show for supporters with Early access on, and open to everyone with 0.7.0. The Korean and Chinese text is machine translated.

## About the betas

Anyone can see the beta. To use it, you get me a coffee on Ko-fi. I try not to lock anyone out of features, and this is the closest way I can get support for the time I put into Folio. Or you can wait for the next release and get everything then.

Even with the betas, I will fix issues without you buying a coffee, no matter what. Keep reporting!

## Verify it

- The release page lists the APK's SHA-256 and the release key's certificate SHA-256, read from the build, and the APK's hash is also in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- VirusTotal: not run for this beta; it is done for the stable release.
- Permissions: see [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md). Beta 5 adds none. Sharing a photo to Folio uses an image share target, not a permission.
