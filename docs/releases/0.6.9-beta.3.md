# Folio 0.6.9-beta.3 for supporters

DRAFT, 14 Oct 2026. Claude's wording from the changelog; McCal rewrites it before it goes anywhere (REL-25).

The third beta of 0.6.9: the new motion finished, Screenshot Mode that can swap apps, and fixes for what beta 1 started.
Version `0.6.9-beta.3`, versionCode 6090 like the earlier betas: a beta shares its release's code, and Software Update
orders betas by name, so beta.3 installs over beta.2, beta.1 and 0.6.8 (6080). Supporters get it the way they got the
others, through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.2

- **Swap apps in Screenshot Mode:** Settings > General > Advanced > Swap Apps shows chosen apps, or every app you
  installed, as the phone's own apps while Screenshot Mode is on, with their icon and name. A tap still opens the real
  app, their widgets show as a plain card, and their menu can't give the swap away.
- **Delete apps from Folio:** press and hold an app on Home and choose Remove App to delete it or only take it off Home,
  or Delete App in the App Library. Android confirms every delete. Apps that came with the phone say only Android can
  delete them. This adds one permission, which asks for nothing and only opens Android's own uninstall screen: see
  [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md).
- **The new motion, finished** (Settings > General > Advanced > New motion, supporters first): a moved icon lifts where
  it is and lands in its new spot; hold it over the middle of an app for a folder, or its edge to make room. Folders and
  app menus grow out of their icon and go back into it. Spotlight, the top panels and Market pages follow a back swipe.
- **Reduce Motion:** Settings > Accessibility > Reduce Motion fades things in where they land instead of sliding or
  growing, a step between all motion and Android's Remove animations.
- **Cloned apps in the App Library:** a second copy of an app, from Dual Messenger or Android's app cloning, sits beside
  the original with Dual under its name.
- Fixes:
  - The Big Clock offers colors with Android's own wallpaper, from the wallpaper's main colors with a soft shade behind
    the time, or the phone's theme colors when Android shares none; Font sits beside Color (#330).
  - The Dynamic Island fits the Fold8's cover camera instead of standing much taller than the lens (#61).
  - Back closes an open folder again, and pulling the dock handle down moves the dock (#326).
  - Create Folder always makes the folder, also from the dock or the App Library on a full page.
  - Apps inside an open folder show their notification badges (#362), and the dock's picker says when an app is
    already in a folder.
  - Updating a tweak keeps the settings you changed, a package whose source pulls your version turns off and says why,
    and a version its source marks broken can't be installed.
  - No drop outlines through widgets while moving an app, Animation Speed reaches the last springs, and more of Folio
    follows your language.

The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is Claude's translation and has not had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Delete apps:** delete one, cancel one, and try an app that came with the phone.
2. **Swap apps** in Screenshot Mode: Home, the dock, the App Library, Search, a widget, and a press and hold.
3. **Moving an icon** with New motion on: into an empty cell, between two apps, and onto an app to make a folder.
4. **Folders and menus** with New motion on: open and close a folder, tap outside an app menu.
5. **A back swipe** over Spotlight, Control Center and a Market page, letting go and changing your mind.
6. **Reduce Motion** on and off, and with Android's Remove animations.
7. **The Big Clock** with Android's own wallpaper: the colors, the shade and each Font.
8. **The island** at the Fold8's cover camera.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this with a merge commit (REL-13).
2. Full checks and the smoke test, then tag `v0.6.9-beta.3`.
3. Sign from the tag with `scripts/release-signed-windows.ps1` and the vault "folio". Betas go to supporters only: never
   a release on the public `folio` repo.
4. Create the pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` as a draft with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`: the APK's SHA-256
   (REL-22), the VirusTotal line (REL-23), and the pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24), which
   matters this time for the delete permission. Publishing the draft posts to #beta, so publish only on McCal's yes.
5. Check Market > Sources > Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
