# Folio 0.6.8-beta.6 for supporters, the release candidate

The sixth beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth", and its release candidate: if nothing in it breaks,
the stable 0.6.8 ships this beta's app with only the version, the dated changelog and the roadmap changed (REL-4b,
checked on the stable's bump, and REL-13). Version `0.6.8-beta.6`, versionCode 6080 like the betas before it, so it
installs over beta.5, beta.4 and 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta. The stable follows after at least a day on this one with
nothing broken (REL-20: #221 changes how the Market installs).

## What's new since beta.5

- **The Market says whether a package will work** (#221, #222, #234): a package's page has a Compatibility card
  (works with your Folio, needs a later Folio, which screens it covers, what it needs or replaces), a list row says
  "Needs Folio X" or "Inner screen only" under its name, and a package that can't be installed on your phone has no
  Get instead of failing after you tap it. A newer version of something you have installs from its own page, with
  Update, and a row whose newer listing Folio can't read still offers Remove.
- **Smoother clips** (#231, #234): Cabinet's, Harborline's and Flipbook's clips play more smoothly, a clip stops when
  it scrolls out of view, and a still screenshot is decoded once.
- **Options for Harborline and Afterglow** (#230): each has an Options group on its page in Settings › Tweaks.
  Harborline: Amount (Subtle, Standard, Strong) and Tick as you slide. Afterglow: Music card and Island sound bars.
  Everything starts as it was, and Reset puts it back.
- **Colored Albums is now Afterglow** (#226, #234): the same tweak, with a new name, in Settings › Tweaks and the
  Market. It keeps its version, so there's no update to tap and nothing you set is reset. Searching "colored albums"
  still finds it.

- **Folio in your language** (#236, #238): in Korean and Chinese, dates read in that language's order (10월 2일
  금요일, 10月2日 星期五) in StandBy, the Big Clock, Lock Cover, the Side Bar and Today View, and Control Center, the
  island, Settings, the setup card, What's New, Lock Cover's buttons, the headphones card and the folder panel are
  translated where they were still English. TalkBack reads the Side Bar, the island rail's controls and the search
  field's clear button in your language too. English only changed where it was wrong ("1 more new feature").

Everything in beta.5 is still here.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **The Market's package pages**: open Cabinet, Harborline, Flipbook and a package you haven't got. Each tweak
   should open with a clip that plays, and the Compatibility card should say what is true for your phone (a package
   for one screen says so, and a package that needs a newer Folio has no Get).
2. **Harborline's and Afterglow's options**: change them, turn the tweak off and on, then Reset. Do they stick, dim
   with the switch and come back to what they were?
3. **In Korean or Chinese** (Settings › General › Language): StandBy, Lock Cover, Control Center and the Side Bar
   should show dates and On or Off in your language, with nothing cut off.
4. Then beta.5's list.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. This bump adds a row to [docs/in-flight.md](../in-flight.md): no app changes merge to `main` until the stable is
   out (REL-4b, REL-6). The stable's bump removes it.
2. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
3. Run the full checks and the scripted smoke test on the Fold8 against the merge, then tag `v0.6.8-beta.6` on it.
4. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh`. The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta may be built
   undated.
5. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since beta.5.
6. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
7. After a day with nothing broken, the stable: this beta's app with the version set to `0.6.8`, the changelog dated
   and the roadmap's 0.6.8 items marked done. The REL-4b check on that bump fails if anything that goes into the APK
   changed after the tag; docs, tools and tests may.
