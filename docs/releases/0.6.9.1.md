# Folio 0.6.9.1

*Written from McCal's own words on 11 Nov 2026. Not signed off yet: read it once before it goes anywhere.*

A hotfix on 0.6.9, out on 10 Nov 2026. Version `0.6.9.1`, versionCode 6091, so it installs over 0.6.9 and anything earlier.

Here are some fixes, and a few small supporter and beta changes that help me and you.

## What's fixed

- **Supporters can now see why betas are missing.** I needed to push some small fixes, and some supporters couldn't tell why they weren't getting new betas. Settings › Software Update now says why, whether it's a code that ended, Early access or Beta Updates being off, or the beta server being out of reach, with a Supporter button beside it.
- **Spotlight and Reduce Motion.** Reduce Motion gives you less movement on screen, for people who get disoriented by it. Spotlight wasn't listening: it still slid and grew into place with Reduce Motion on. It now only fades in and out.
- **App Library and folding.** Folio could close on its own when the window changed size, like folding or unfolding the phone, during an App Library search. It now waits for the screen to settle before clearing the search.

## About the betas

Anyone can see the beta. To use it, you get me a coffee on Ko-fi. I try not to lock anyone out of features, and this is the closest way I can get support for the time I put into Folio. Or you can wait a month or so for the next release, and you get everything then.

This hotfix matters for another reason. Even with the betas, I will fix issues without you buying a coffee, no matter what. Keep reporting!
