# Folio 0.7.0 beta 4

*Released 14 Nov 2026 as a pre-release on folio-beta. Checked against the changelog and the manifest before it went out.*

Beta 4 is about making Control Center and Home yours: move, resize, add and remove controls, and undo and redo on Home.

## What's new

- **Edit Control Center.** In its edit mode, press and hold a control to move it, drag its corner to resize it, and use the minus to take it off. Drop a control on another and they trade places. Places and sizes are saved, and Reset puts the usual page back. On a window too short for everything, the page scrolls so every control and module can still be moved and resized.
- **Add a Control, in sections.** The gallery is searchable and grouped under Connectivity, Display and sound, Tools, Wallet and security, Accessibility and System. Every module can be taken off and added back.
- **Nine more controls.** Screen Mirroring, Display, Accessibility, Recorder, Location and VPN open the phone's own page for each. Sound mode steps Ring, Vibrate and Silent with a tap (Silent needs Do Not Disturb access, which the tile asks for). Timeout steps the screen timeout from 15 seconds to 10 minutes (it needs Modify system settings, which Rotation Lock and brightness already use). Night Light opens its page. A tile that holds a value shows it right on the tile.
- **Hold cards.** Press and hold brightness or volume for a card with Auto brightness, or Media, Ring and Alarm sliders. On a wide screen Control Center can sit on the right, in the middle, or on the left.
- **Undo and redo on Home.** Two buttons beside Edit undo and redo your last change. The Undo pill fades away after a few seconds and sits above the dock.
- **A calmer widget menu.** Holding a widget shows its sizes and everyday actions first, Remove stays at the bottom, and custom size, pages and stack order are under More options.
- **Photos widget.** Pick up to 20 photos and Home shows them one at a time with a soft fade and a play and pause button. Tap it for Show the date, Shuffle and a speed of 5, 10 or 30 seconds, or hold it for Customize and Remove this photo. With Reduce Motion on, photos switch without a fade.
- **Wi-Fi name on the status rail.** Long names slide sideways to show all of it. Android gives a network name only to apps with precise location, so there is a **Show Wi-Fi network name** row in Settings › Notifications & Control Center that asks for it. Folio reads just the name, never where you are, and without the permission the rail says Wi-Fi as before.
- **Seek in Now Playing.** Tap or drag along the progress line on the Music tile or the Music widget to move the track.
- **Search the Market.** The Packages tab has a search box.
- **Tips.** Settings › General › Tips lists every new feature, newest first.

## Fixed

- Folding or unfolding with Notification Center or Control Center open no longer closes Folio (a "Padding must be non-negative" crash reported on a Flip3).
- On a short window, Control Center's Add a Control card no longer covers the last row, the minus badges no longer cover small tiles' icons, and Home's Edit bar no longer shows two Add or Done buttons.
- Bug reports now name the exact build, how long Folio had run, and each widget on Home, so a report says what failed.

## Please try first

Edit Control Center: add a control, resize one, drop one onto another, and try the new Sound mode and Timeout tiles. Tell me if anything snaps back, clips or covers something, or if a press and hold removes a control instead of lifting it.

Control Center editing and the Photos widget are early-access features: they show for supporters with Early access on, and open to everyone with 0.7.0. The Korean and Chinese text is machine translated.
