# Folio 0.7.0 beta 4

*DRAFT, not released. Drafted from the changelog and the commits since beta 3; McCal edits the voice before it ships.*

Beta 4 is about making Control Center and Home yours: you can now move, resize, add and remove controls, and edit Home with undo and redo.

## What's new

- **Edit Control Center.** Hold and edit: move a control, resize it from its corner, remove it with the minus badge, and add more from **Add a Control**, which is grouped (Connectivity, Display and sound, Tools, Wallet and security, Accessibility, System) and searchable. Over other apps, Control Center uses the layout you made.
- **Six more controls.** Screen Mirroring, Display, Accessibility, Recorder, Location and VPN open the phone's own page for each.
- **Control Center pages.** Swipe the page strip to change category. On a wide screen the panel can sit compact on the side you choose, and Focus has a small bar of its own.
- **Edit Home with undo and redo.** Two buttons beside Edit undo and redo your last change. Unfolded, Add, Edit, Undo and Redo sit at the top of the page, and Done sits on the other side of the media island when the island is in the way.
- **A calmer widget menu.** Holding a widget shows its sizes and everyday actions first, Remove stays at the bottom, and the rest is under More options.
- **Photos widget.** Pick up to 20 photos and Home shows them one at a time with a soft fade. Hold it to customize or remove a photo. With Reduce Motion on the photos switch without a fade.
- **Wi-Fi name and carrier on the status rail.** Long names slide sideways to show all of it. The Wi-Fi name needs precise location, so there is a **Show Wi-Fi network name** row in Settings › Notifications & Control Center that asks for it. It is off until you turn it on, and Folio reads just the name, never where you are.
- **Seek in Now Playing.** Tap or drag the progress line on the Music tile or widget to move the track.
- **Battery in the status rail.** Gauge is the default style, with a number that rolls, a low-battery breath, and a charging sweep. The charging bolt is an option.
- **Search the Market.** The Packages tab has a search box.
- **Tips.** Settings › General › Tips lists every new feature, newest first.

## Fixed

- Folding or unfolding with Notification Center, Control Center or a sheet (Settings, the widget picker) open no longer crashes.
- Unfolded Home no longer leaves the edge of the previous page showing on the left after you turn a page.
- Edit mode keeps the last row clear of the bottom controls, and the widget picker is no longer see-through.
- Crash reports now name the build, the widgets on Home and the process, so a report says what failed.
