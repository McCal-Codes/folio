# Folio 0.7.0-beta.2 for supporters

The second beta of 0.7.0. Version `0.7.0-beta.2`, versionCode 7000, so it installs cleanly over 0.7.0-beta.1, 0.6.9, and earlier versions, and stable 0.7.0 will install over it. Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta.

## What's new

- **Dynamic Island notification tint & rolling ticker:** Notification popup backgrounds in the dynamic island now subtly adapt to the sending app's icon color palette. A new rolling ticker setting smoothly scrolls notification text along the island pill instead of expanding into a card.
- **App shortcut menu palette tint:** Home screen and Cabinet app context menus adopt a frosted glass background tinted with the app icon's color palette (e.g. emerald glass for Google Play Store).
- **Seamless unfolded two-page spacing:** When unfolded flat, the left and right pages sit closer together for a continuous two-page layout, and smoothly separate away from the hinge crease when partly folded.
- **New StandBy clock personalities:** Vintage warm orange Nixie Tube filaments with realistic glass reflections, and Minimalist typography with hairline divider and date capsule.
- **StandBy interactive widget stack & vertical gestures:** Tap or vertically swipe/flick the widget card in StandBy to cycle between Up Next Calendar, Month Grid, Weather Outlook, and Device Vitals with directional slide animations, animated dots, and haptic ticks.
- **Dual-screen ambient lamp:** Bedside lamp mode softly illuminates the secondary outer screen with a warm breathing glow matching your clock color while folded or propped in StandBy.
- **StandBy live preview in Settings:** Responsive, scalable preview card in Settings showing the active clock face, color accents, and interactive widget cycling without clipping.
- **Unfolded leading page widget stabilization:** Preserved saveable state and improved layout hysteresis for widgets on the left screen of foldables, preventing unmounting or re-creation during page swipes.
- **Page-edge resistance & overscroll elasticity:** Pulling past the first Home page or past App Library provides smooth physics resistance that springs back upon release.
- **Temporary widget expansion:** Long-press any built-in widget (Clock, Calendar, Vitals) to inspect an expanded interactive preview card and launch related actions without altering your saved layout.
- **Contextual rail editing & immediate undo:** Editing Home surfaces contextual Add Widget, Undo, and Done buttons directly in the sidebar rail, paired with a floating immediate undo chip.
- **Fold displacement for folder panels:** Open folders on dual-screen or foldable displays cleanly settle onto a single display half rather than straddling the hinge crease.
- **Quiet charging response & case-fit edge clearance:** Connecting power triggers a gentle pulse and soft glow on the battery indicator. A new case-fit clearance setting adds adjustable edge insets for thick phone cases.
- **Duo-styled status rail & charging indicators:** Tabular numerals prevent time jitter as minutes advance, and a compact lightning bolt indicator sits beside the battery percentage/gauge when on power.
- **Control Center toggle refinements:** Active color accents on Battery Saver, in-place system sheet support for the Bluetooth tile, and fallback package launching for calculators and timers.
- **Polished What's New & Build Changelog sheets:** Changelog and dev build welcome sheets render entries in frosted glass cards with contextual feature icons and clean commit hashes instead of wrapping raw text.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Dynamic Island notification tint & rolling ticker**: send a notification with Island popup enabled; observe the island background taking a subtle tint of the app's icon palette. Turn on "Rolling notification ticker" to see messages roll across the pill.
2. **App shortcut menu palette tint**: long-press or swipe up on an app on Home (e.g. Play Store, Settings); check the frosted liquid glass tint matching the app's icon.
3. **Seamless unfolded two-page spacing**: unfold your foldable phone flat and observe the narrower, unified spacing between left and right pages; partly fold into book posture to see them separate away from the crease.
4. **Nixie & Minimal StandBy clocks**: in Settings › Fold & Displays › StandBy › Clock Style, select Nixie or Minimalist and verify layout in day and night modes.
5. **StandBy widget carousel**: tap the widget card in StandBy to cycle between Calendar, Month Grid, Weather Outlook, and Battery/RAM vitals.
6. **Dual-screen ambient lamp**: verify the ambient glow on the secondary outer display when propped or folded in tent posture.
7. **Left-screen widget stability**: verify widgets placed on the left side of unfolded foldables remain responsive without tearing or re-creating when swiping pages.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
