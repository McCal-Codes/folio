# Folio 0.6.8-beta.5 for supporters

The fifth beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth". It was cut as the release candidate, but `main` moved
on before it was published, so beta.6 is the release candidate now (step 6). Version `0.6.8-beta.5`, tagged on
198a446, versionCode 6080 like the betas before it, so it installs over beta.4 and over 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta, after beta.4 (published 2 Oct).

## What's new since beta.4

- **Back in Settings from the Folio icon** (#224): opened from the Folio icon or Android's settings gear, Settings is
  a tab beside the Market, and there Back closed everything from any page. It goes up a page now, as it does in
  Settings opened from Home, and closes Settings only from the top.
- **TalkBack in Settings** (#224): the search field is read as "Search", rows on the phone no longer each say "Not
  selected", and the preview of Home at the top is one picture instead of about 25 of its parts.
- **Screenshots in the Market** (#223, #225): Cabinet, Harborline and Flipbook show real screenshots on their Market
  pages instead of "No screenshots", as short clips that loop while they are on screen. With Reduce Motion on, a clip
  stays on its first frame.
- **0.6.8's features are open on this build** (#217): clearing an app's badges when it opens, StandBy's charging,
  tent and lock screen ways, and page effects. Supporters already had them, so nothing changes on their phones; it
  is what makes this the release candidate, because the stable has to be this tree.

Everything in beta.4 is still here.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Back in Settings from the Folio icon**: open Folio from its icon, go to Settings › General › Backup, and press
   Back three times. It should go to General, then the Settings list, then close.
2. **TalkBack in Settings**, if you use it: the search field should say "Search", the rows shouldn't say "Not
   selected", and the Home preview should be one stop.
3. **The Market's tweak pages**: Cabinet, Harborline and Flipbook should each open with a clip that loops while it is
   on screen, and holds still with Reduce Motion on.
4. Then beta.4's list, StandBy on the lock screen first.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.5` on its head.
3. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh`. The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta may be built
   undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since beta.4.
5. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
6. The stable doesn't follow this beta. `main` moved on after the tag (#221, #222, #226, #230 and #231 change the
   app), and McCal made beta.6 the release candidate instead (2 Oct): see [0.6.8-beta.6](0.6.8-beta.6.md).
