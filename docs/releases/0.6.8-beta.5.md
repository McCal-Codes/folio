# Folio 0.6.8-beta.5 for supporters, the release candidate

The fifth beta of 0.6.8, "Your language, your Home, and Smooth", and its release candidate: if nothing in it breaks,
the stable 0.6.8 is this tree with only the version changed (REL-4b, REL-13). Version `0.6.8-beta.5`, versionCode
6080 like the betas before it, so it installs over beta.4 and over 0.6.7.3 (6073).

Supporters get it through Market › Sources › Folio Beta, after beta.4 (published 2 Oct). The stable follows after at
least a day on this one with nothing broken (REL-20).

## What's new since beta.4

- **The Market says whether a package will work** (#221, #222): a package's page has a Compatibility card (works with
  your Folio, needs a later Folio, which screens it covers, what it needs or replaces), a list row says "Needs Folio
  X" or "Inner screen only" under its name, and a package that can't be installed on your phone has no Get instead of
  failing after you tap it. A newer version of something you have installs from its own page now, with Update.
- **Screenshots that move** (#223, #225, #231): Cabinet, Harborline and Flipbook show short looping clips on their
  Market pages, the panel opening, the dock swelling under a finger, a page turning. With Reduce Motion on, a clip
  stays on its first frame.
- **Options for Harborline and Afterglow** (#230): each has an Options group on its page in Settings › Tweaks.
  Harborline: Amount (Subtle, Standard, Strong) and Tick as you slide. Afterglow: Music card and Island sound bars.
  Everything starts as it was, and Reset puts it back.
- **Colored Albums is now Afterglow** (#226): the same tweak, with a new name, in Settings › Tweaks and the Market.
  Searching "colored albums" still finds it.
- **Back in Settings from the Folio icon** (#224): opened from the Folio icon or Android's settings gear, Settings is
  a tab beside the Market, and there Back closed everything from any page. It goes up a page now, as it does in
  Settings opened from Home, and closes Settings only from the top.
- **TalkBack in Settings** (#224): the search field is read as "Search", rows on the phone no longer each say "Not
  selected", and the preview of Home at the top is one picture instead of about 25 of its parts.
- **0.6.8's features are open on this build** (#217): clearing an app's badges when it opens, StandBy's charging,
  tent and lock screen ways, and page effects. Supporters already had them, so nothing changes on their phones; it
  is what makes this the release candidate, because the stable has to be this tree.

Everything in beta.4 is still here.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **The Market's package pages**: open Cabinet, Harborline, Flipbook and a package you haven't got. Each tweak
   should open with a clip that plays, and the Compatibility card should say what is true for your phone (a package
   for one screen says so, and a package that needs a newer Folio has no Get).
2. **Harborline's and Afterglow's options**: change them, turn the tweak off and on, then Reset. Do they stick, dim
   with the switch and come back to what they were?
3. **Back in Settings from the Folio icon**: open Folio from its icon, go to Settings › General › Backup, and press
   Back three times. It should go to General, then the Settings list, then close.
4. **TalkBack in Settings**, if you use it: the search field should say "Search", the rows shouldn't say "Not
   selected", and the Home preview should be one stop.
5. Then beta.4's list, StandBy on the lock screen first.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.

## Steps

1. Merge this bump with a merge commit.
2. Tag `v0.6.8-beta.5` on its head.
3. Build and sign with `scripts/release-signed-keychain.sh`. The changelog stays `Unreleased`: a beta may be built
   undated.
4. Publish as a pre-release on `McCal-Codes/folio-beta` with the APK and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. In the message that carries
   the link, give the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report with the usual heads-up about small engines
   (REL-23), and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24). No new permission since beta.4.
5. Check that Market › Sources › Folio Beta offers it on a supporter's phone.
6. After a day with nothing broken, the stable: this tree with only the version (`0.6.8`) and the dates changed (the
   changelog dated, the roadmap's 0.6.8 items marked done; REL-4b, REL-13). Nothing else merges to `main` in between; anything that
   must go in goes out as another beta first (REL-4b, REL-16).
