# Folio 0.7.0 beta 6

*Pre-release on folio-beta. Checked against the changelog and the manifest before it went out.*

Beta 6 is about Home pages you can see and arrange, a wallpaper of your own color, a widget menu that stays beside the widget, a Control Center editor that uses a wide screen, a longer and smoother fold effect, and a snapping fold that shows when something is loading. It also makes bug reports carry the facts, calms a Fold whose hinge sensor misreads, and moves StandBy 2 into the Market with real captures on tweak pages.

## What's new

- **Page overview.** Every Home page as a small picture in a grid, opened from the page dots (long-press them while icons wiggle) or from Page overview on the Edit menu. Tick the circle under a page to show it on Home or untick it to hide it. A hidden page keeps its apps and stays in the overview, dimmed, and the last page showing cannot be hidden. Hold a page and drag it to move it, or hold Alt and press an arrow key, or use Move page earlier and Move page later in TalkBack's actions. Widgets, page looks and Focus pages follow the page, and Undo takes a move or a hide back. With seven or more pages the "3 / 9" counter opens the overview too. Nothing flashes, and Reduce Motion fades the screen in.
- **The widget menu is a small popover.** Holding a widget opens a compact menu right beside it instead of a big dark sheet, in the same style as an app icon's menu: Edit Widget, Size (with a checkmark on the current size and a new Extra Large, 4 × 6, for widgets that allow it), Replace Widget, Make a Stack, Edit Home Screen, More and, in red at the bottom, Remove Widget. Custom Size, Move to Page, the Photos widget's Remove This Photo and a stack's order are behind More. Reduce Motion fades it in instead of growing it.
- **The fold effect.** Duet's focus now takes a little under a second to clear after the screen lights, on the open screen and on the cover, about as long as the Duo's own, where it used to be about half a second. The half that folds away keeps a little of its own picture, soft and dim, near the hinge instead of going flat black, and it still goes fully dark at its outer edge. The screen no longer shrinks during the fold, so the thin outline of Android's own window color around it is gone, and the fold preview in Settings matches. The effect also no longer skips ahead just after the screen lights: it picks up where it paused, so the fade stays smooth and ends only a moment later. Where your phone reports a smooth hinge angle, the angle still sets the pace. With Reduce Motion on, the fade keeps its shorter length and the effect is a plain shade. Intensity still scales the whole effect and nothing flashes. Timing on the Fold8 has been checked on the emulator only.
- **A snapping fold for loading.** The two halves of a Fold snap shut with a small click and a faint ring, then spring open again, over and over. It replaces the plain spinner on the widget list while it loads, on a widget preview that is not ready, on a Market package that is queued and on a Home widget waiting for you to finish its setup. Nothing flashes: the ring is faint and fades in over two frames. Reduce Motion keeps the halves still and fades them slowly, and Animation Speed changes how fast it loops.
- **The Photos widget turns over for Edit Widget.** Its options (change photos, date, shuffle, speed) now sit on the back of the widget, a card with Done, instead of in a dialog. The widget turns like a card, and fades with Reduce Motion.
- **The wallpaper preview shows your Home.** The full-screen preview for a wallpaper or a photo you are framing now draws your own icons, widgets and dock over the picture, as Home will look, instead of stand-in boxes. A wide screen shows two pages. It is a still picture, nothing moves.
- **Folio's own wallpapers.** Moon Hills and Ember Hills are soft layered hills in Folio Teal, drawn for Folio. A new install now starts on Moon Hills instead of a bare grey surface. If you have used Folio before, your wallpaper does not change, and both pieces stay in the list.
- **Recolor Folio's own wallpapers.** In the wallpaper preview, Customize opens hue, saturation and lightness sliders for everything at once or for just the sky, the hills or the glow, with a few starting colors and a reset. The picture follows your finger: dragging Hue, Saturation or Lightness redraws it right away and melts from one color to the next, where it used to lag and step. The panel behind the sliders is also darker, so its labels stay readable over a bright moon. Set keeps it. Still pictures, nothing moves or flashes.
- **Blur and Dim sliders for a photo wallpaper.** The framing screen's Blur switch is now a Blur slider with a Dim slider beside it. Both change the picture behind Home while you drag, and Set saves the picture the way you left it. Blur used to be on or off, with one fixed strength.
- **Sliders are easier to see on dark screens.** The unfilled part of a slider's track is brighter, so you can see how far a slider goes before you touch it.
- **Four new wallpapers.** Sage Dunes, Ember Dusk, Sand Grain and Teal Horizon, minimal and soft like the others, drawn for Folio and credited under its MIT License in the wallpaper picker. They are still pictures, nothing moves or flashes.
- **Two-pane Control Center editor.** On an unfolded Fold, a tablet or any window about 640 dp wide, your layout stands on the left with the same minus badges, held moves and resize corners, and Add a Control is a list beside it with a search field, the sections you know and a teal plus on every row. On a Fold the divider is the crease, so no control sits across the fold. Folded, on a phone or half open, it works as before. TalkBack reads the layout first, then the list.
- **Control Center controls arrive together.** Opening Control Center now brings its controls in small, soft and see-through, growing to full size and sharpening as they land, almost all at once and settled in about a quarter of a second, instead of rising one after another. Edit mode and Reduce Motion are unchanged (no motion). Nothing flashes.
- **Simple Mode preview.** A calm Home for someone who wants a simpler phone, set up by a helper, under Settings > Accessibility > Experience > Simple or "Setting this up for someone else?" during setup. It shows a big labeled list or grid of up to six apps, with the time, date and battery on top, and a helper can add up to four people to call, picked from the contact picker. Tapping one opens the dialer with their number, so Folio needs no Contacts or call permission. Helper Settings opens with the phone's screen lock and changes the people, the apps, the style and the text size. Safe Mode shows Folio's usual Home so Simple can never trap anyone, and leaving brings your usual Home back exactly. Supporters on the beta see it first.
- **Bug reports arrive with the facts.** Report a Bug on GitHub now opens a form with the Screen already picked and a short summary in Diagnostics: your phone and screen, where Home was, the last few events and the top of the latest crash. Nothing is sent until you submit the form. The full report also says where Home was and which Market packages are installed, and Email a Report, Share Diagnostics and What to Test can attach up to three screenshots you choose with the system photo picker. They go only with the email or share.
- **TalkBack reads badges.** An app icon with a badge now says its count with its name, such as "Messages, 5 notifications", or "has notifications" when the badge is a dot. With badges off, it says only the name.
- **More in Settings > Help.** Help now has Suggest a Feature and Help Translate (each opens its GitHub form in your browser), Known Issues (the open bugs), and Release Notes (the same What's New screen as Tips), in groups called Tell Us and Look Around, with Show Welcome Again on its own below. What to Test moves into Look Around on beta builds. Nothing is sent until you submit a form yourself.
- **Market pages show real captures.** The Cabinet, Harborline, Roll Call and Palette pages now show the tweak in use on a real phone instead of a drawing, and a page's picture is shown whole at its own shape instead of being cropped to a strip. The other tweak pages follow as they are captured.
- **StandBy 2 is a package in the Market.** It has its own page in the Folio source, like Cabinet, Harborline, Roll Call, Palette, Afterglow, Flipbook and Duet, so the Market lists it with its own page and version. A phone that already has StandBy 2 on keeps it on with its own look, and removing the package puts StandBy 2 back as it was. StandBy itself works as before.
- **Keyd is listed for everyone.** Once the Market is open to everyone, Keyd's source is already in Sources, added once with its key built into Folio, so the keyboard can be tried without a supporter code. Forgetting it is final, and a code coming off no longer takes it away.
- **Display aids in Folio Dev, and no passphrase on Developer.** For my own Folio Dev build only: a card that shows where Show taps, Pointer location and three other Android developer switches stand, and a Developer page that opens on a warning instead of asking for a passphrase. Nothing here shows in the beta or the release build.

## Fixed

- **The cover screen's fold effect is smooth with Duo Clip.** After you fold the phone shut, the cover now comes into focus gradually over the last part of the fade, as the open screen does. It used to stay dark and frosted for most of the fade and then lose all of its blur in a few frames, which looked choppy. Opening the phone from the cover now frosts it over several frames instead of all at once, and on both screens the last of the blur no longer drops away in a single frame at the end of a fade. The fade lasts as long as before, and nothing flashes.
- **Control Center's side rail no longer overlaps.** On the unfolded inner screen, the page buttons beside the controls stood in the middle of the rail, so the first one sat under the cellular signal mark and against the Wi-Fi name. They now stay in the middle only while there is room, and otherwise start just under the battery, Wi-Fi and cellular marks. Where the rail cannot hold the marks and every page button, such as at the largest text size on a short window, the marks are left out.
- **Control Center and Notification Center no longer open on a sharp Home.** The blurred picture behind a panel is taken once the panel has begun to open, so the tiles could appear over a crisp Home for a few frames before the blur caught up. Frosted glass now holds the place until the picture is ready and fades out as it fades in, with no step. Checked on the emulator only; nothing flashes.
- **Two small motion fixes.** The row Settings search takes you to now glows in over a moment and fades, where it used to switch on at full strength, and the Search Google Without AI sheet fades in without sliding when Reduce Motion is on.
- **Simple Mode keeps a picked person.** Adding a person to call opened the contact picker, and Helper Settings locked itself when Folio stopped for it, so you landed on Home and the person was never saved. Helper Settings now stays open for the pick, and its 5-minute idle lock waits while the picker is open, for up to 3 minutes. Turning the screen off while it is open locks it at once.
- **Home flickering when the fold sensor misreads.** On a Fold whose hinge sensor kept reporting "folded" while the phone was open, Home kept blurring and dimming on its own and the screen kept waking. The first false reading still plays and repeats are ignored for a while, longer each time. A real fold is never ignored.
- **Spotlight fits a short window.** On a flip's cover screen or a phone on its side, opening Spotlight no longer draws a Suggestions card half under the keyboard. With room for one row you get a row of app icons, and with less than that the search field stands alone. A regular phone keeps the whole card.
- **Today View ends above the Preview bar.** Before Folio is your Home app, the last widget on Today View scrolled to rest under "Use as Home" on a short window such as a flip's cover screen. It now stops above the bar. Once Folio is the Home app nothing moves.

## Please try first

If you have a Fold, leave it open on Home for a while and tell me if Home blurs, dims or flickers by itself, then fold it for real and check the fold still plays. Next, open Page overview: hide a page, show it again, drag one to a new place and tap Undo. Play the fold effect a few times, on the open screen and on the cover, and tell me if it ever skips, shows an outline or feels too long. Open the widget list, or add a widget that needs setup, and watch the snapping fold load. After that, hold a widget and try the new menu, then pick Moon Hills in the wallpaper picker, open Customize and move the sliders, and tell me if the preview feels slow or the colors look wrong anywhere. Open Control Center a few times, with Reduce Motion off and then on, and tell me if the controls ever pop, jump or flash. If you use TalkBack, check that badges are read and that the Page overview actions are offered. If you have a flip, open Spotlight and Today View on the cover screen and tell me if anything still sits under the keyboard or the Preview bar. If StandBy 2 was on for you, check it still looks the same with the new package. Finally, send a bug report from Settings and tell me if the form is missing something it should have.

The Control Center editor and the Photos widget are early-access features: they show for supporters with Early access on, and open to everyone with 0.7.0. Simple Mode is an early preview in the same way, and it opens to everyone later than 0.7.0. The Korean and Chinese text is machine translated.

## About the betas

Anyone can see the beta. To use it, you get me a coffee on Ko-fi. I try not to lock anyone out of features, and this is the closest way I can get support for the time I put into Folio. Or you can wait for the next release and get everything then.

Even with the betas, I will fix issues without you buying a coffee, no matter what. Keep reporting!

## Verify it

- The release page lists the APK's SHA-256 and the release key's certificate SHA-256, read from the build, and the APK's hash is also in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- VirusTotal: not run for this beta; it is done for the stable release.
- Permissions: see [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md). Beta 6 adds none. Attaching screenshots to a bug report uses the system photo picker, not a permission.
