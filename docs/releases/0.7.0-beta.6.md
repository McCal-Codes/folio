# Folio 0.7.0 beta 6

*Pre-release on folio-beta. Checked against the changelog and the manifest before it went out.*

Beta 6 is about Home pages you can see and arrange, a wallpaper of your own color, a widget menu that stays beside the widget, and a Control Center editor that uses a wide screen. It also makes bug reports carry the facts, calms a Fold whose hinge sensor misreads, and moves StandBy 2 into the Market with real captures on tweak pages.

## What's new

- **Page overview.** Every Home page as a small picture in a grid, opened from the page dots (long-press them while icons wiggle) or from Page overview on the Edit menu. Tick the circle under a page to show it on Home or untick it to hide it. A hidden page keeps its apps and stays in the overview, dimmed, and the last page showing cannot be hidden. Hold a page and drag it to move it, or hold Alt and press an arrow key, or use Move page earlier and Move page later in TalkBack's actions. Widgets, page looks and Focus pages follow the page, and Undo takes a move or a hide back. With seven or more pages the "3 / 9" counter opens the overview too. Nothing flashes, and Reduce Motion fades the screen in.
- **The widget menu is a small popover.** Holding a widget opens a compact menu right beside it instead of a big dark sheet, in the same style as an app icon's menu: Edit Widget, Size (with a checkmark on the current size and a new Extra Large, 4 × 6, for widgets that allow it), Replace Widget, Make a Stack, Edit Home Screen, More and, in red at the bottom, Remove Widget. Custom Size, Move to Page, the Photos widget's Remove This Photo and a stack's order are behind More. Reduce Motion fades it in instead of growing it.
- **The wallpaper preview shows your Home.** The full-screen preview for a wallpaper or a photo you are framing now draws your own icons, widgets and dock over the picture, as Home will look, instead of stand-in boxes. A wide screen shows two pages. It is a still picture, nothing moves.
- **Folio's own wallpapers.** Moon Hills and Ember Hills are soft layered hills in Folio Teal, drawn for Folio. A new install now starts on Moon Hills instead of a bare grey surface. If you have used Folio before, your wallpaper does not change, and both pieces stay in the list.
- **Recolor Folio's own wallpapers.** In the wallpaper preview, Customize opens hue, saturation and lightness sliders for everything at once or for just the sky, the hills or the glow, with a few starting colors and a reset. The picture follows your finger, and Set keeps it. Still pictures, nothing moves or flashes.
- **Four new wallpapers.** Sage Dunes, Ember Dusk, Sand Grain and Teal Horizon, minimal and soft like the others, drawn for Folio and credited under its MIT License in the wallpaper picker. They are still pictures, nothing moves or flashes.
- **Two-pane Control Center editor.** On an unfolded Fold, a tablet or any window about 640 dp wide, your layout stands on the left with the same minus badges, held moves and resize corners, and Add a Control is a list beside it with a search field, the sections you know and a teal plus on every row. On a Fold the divider is the crease, so no control sits across the fold. Folded, on a phone or half open, it works as before. TalkBack reads the layout first, then the list.
- **Control Center controls arrive together.** Opening Control Center now brings its controls in small, soft and see-through, growing to full size and sharpening as they land, almost all at once and settled in about a quarter of a second, instead of rising one after another. Edit mode and Reduce Motion are unchanged (no motion). Nothing flashes.
- **Bug reports arrive with the facts.** Report a Bug on GitHub now opens a form with the Screen already picked and a short summary in Diagnostics: your phone and screen, where Home was, the last few events and the top of the latest crash. Nothing is sent until you submit the form. The full report also says where Home was and which Market packages are installed, and Email a Report, Share Diagnostics and What to Test can attach up to three screenshots you choose with the system photo picker. They go only with the email or share.
- **TalkBack reads badges.** An app icon with a badge now says its count with its name, such as "Messages, 5 notifications", or "has notifications" when the badge is a dot. With badges off, it says only the name.
- **More in Settings > Help.** Help now has Suggest a Feature and Help Translate (each opens its GitHub form in your browser), Known Issues (the open bugs), and Release Notes (the same What's New screen as Tips), in groups called Tell Us and Look Around, with Show Welcome Again on its own below. What to Test moves into Look Around on beta builds. Nothing is sent until you submit a form yourself.
- **Market pages show real captures.** The Cabinet, Harborline, Roll Call and Palette pages now show the tweak in use on a real phone instead of a drawing, and a page's picture is shown whole at its own shape instead of being cropped to a strip. The other tweak pages follow as they are captured.
- **StandBy 2 is a package in the Market.** It has its own page in the Folio source, like Cabinet, Harborline, Roll Call, Palette, Afterglow, Flipbook and Duet, so the Market lists it with its own page and version. A phone that already has StandBy 2 on keeps it on with its own look, and removing the package puts StandBy 2 back as it was. StandBy itself works as before.
- **Keyd is listed for everyone.** Once the Market is open to everyone, Keyd's source is already in Sources, added once with its key built into Folio, so the keyboard can be tried without a supporter code. Forgetting it is final, and a code coming off no longer takes it away.
- **Display aids in Folio Dev, and no passphrase on Developer.** For my own Folio Dev build only: a card that shows where Show taps, Pointer location and three other Android developer switches stand, and a Developer page that opens on a warning instead of asking for a passphrase. Nothing here shows in the beta or the release build.

## Fixed

- **Home flickering when the fold sensor misreads.** On a Fold whose hinge sensor kept reporting "folded" while the phone was open, Home kept blurring and dimming on its own and the screen kept waking. The first false reading still plays and repeats are ignored for a while, longer each time. A real fold is never ignored.
- **Spotlight fits a short window.** On a flip's cover screen or a phone on its side, opening Spotlight no longer draws a Suggestions card half under the keyboard. With room for one row you get a row of app icons, and with less than that the search field stands alone. A regular phone keeps the whole card.
- **Today View ends above the Preview bar.** Before Folio is your Home app, the last widget on Today View scrolled to rest under "Use as Home" on a short window such as a flip's cover screen. It now stops above the bar. Once Folio is the Home app nothing moves.

## Please try first

If you have a Fold, leave it open on Home for a while and tell me if Home blurs, dims or flickers by itself, then fold it for real and check the fold still plays. Next, open Page overview: hide a page, show it again, drag one to a new place and tap Undo. After that, hold a widget and try the new menu, then pick Moon Hills in the wallpaper picker, open Customize and move the sliders, and tell me if the preview feels slow or the colors look wrong anywhere. Open Control Center a few times, with Reduce Motion off and then on, and tell me if the controls ever pop, jump or flash. If you use TalkBack, check that badges are read and that the Page overview actions are offered. If you have a flip, open Spotlight and Today View on the cover screen and tell me if anything still sits under the keyboard or the Preview bar. If StandBy 2 was on for you, check it still looks the same with the new package. Finally, send a bug report from Settings and tell me if the form is missing something it should have.

The Control Center editor and the Photos widget are early-access features: they show for supporters with Early access on, and open to everyone with 0.7.0. The Korean and Chinese text is machine translated.

## About the betas

Anyone can see the beta. To use it, you get me a coffee on Ko-fi. I try not to lock anyone out of features, and this is the closest way I can get support for the time I put into Folio. Or you can wait for the next release and get everything then.

Even with the betas, I will fix issues without you buying a coffee, no matter what. Keep reporting!

## Verify it

- The release page lists the APK's SHA-256 and the release key's certificate SHA-256, read from the build, and the APK's hash is also in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- VirusTotal: not run for this beta; it is done for the stable release.
- Permissions: see [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md). Beta 6 adds none. Attaching screenshots to a bug report uses the system photo picker, not a permission.
