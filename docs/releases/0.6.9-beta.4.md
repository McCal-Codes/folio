# Folio 0.6.9-beta.4 for supporters

DRAFT, 16 Oct 2026. Claude's wording from the changelog and merged PRs; McCal rewrites it before it goes anywhere (REL-25).
Items still in an open PR when this was written are marked and must come out or be updated before the tag.

**Smooth, and safe to change.**

Thank you for testing. This beta is about two things: the island and Home feeling smoother and calmer, and your layout being
safer when you change it or restore it. The island sits level and does what you expect when you tap or swipe it, the Home
button finally takes you home, and a round of fixes means a cancelled drag, a restore or a stuck blur no longer leaves you
with a mess. A few small extras ride along too. If something feels wrong, tell us which phone and screen you were on.

Version `0.6.9-beta.4`, versionCode 6090 like the earlier betas: a beta shares its release's code, and Software Update orders
betas by name, so beta.4 installs over beta.3, beta.2, beta.1 and 0.6.8 (6080). Supporters get it through Market > Sources >
Folio Beta.

## What's new since beta.3

- **A calmer island:** the island sits at the same height on Home and over apps, on the cover and the unfolded screen. Tap
  outside its card to close it, and it no longer opens again by itself after a message or call. It follows Reduce Motion
  (the bars stand still), and TalkBack reads whether the card is open.
- **Swipe a call away:** swipe a ringing call's card up and it shrinks to the pill; the call keeps ringing, and a tap on the
  pill brings the card back.
- **Island on the unfolded screen:** Hide in Landscape no longer hides the island on the unfolded screen unless you turn on
  Also on the Unfolded Screen.
- **Home button on Home:** pressing Home while you are already on Home goes to the first page. Coming back from an app
  still returns you to the page you left.
- **Clear Suggestions History:** Settings > Search & App Library. Folio forgets what it learned from your app use and your
  recent apps; Android's own usage data is not changed.
- **App Library:** search starts at the top, clears when you leave, and no longer flashes the A to Z list while the
  category tiles load.
- **Spotlight launches stay in front** when Discover is the left page.
- **Big Clock:** one color dot per color you can tell apart, and tinted icons' Wallpaper color uses the palette too.

## Fixes

- A cancelled drag puts the app back where it was.
- Home no longer stays blurred behind nothing after a sheet or the new-build page.
- Layout Backup: Restore on the damaged-layout notice restores, a backup lists only the apps the layout uses, Market
  packages in a backup are checked before they turn on, and an old Smart Stack no longer lands on a different widget.
- Safe Mode stays on until you leave it, and a newer Home layout says so.
- Settings opens on the layout you are in, and upright rows are measured separately.
- A Focus turned off by hand stays off after a restart; Smart Rotate counts time away from Home.
- Small fixes: Icon Actions that open a missing app say so, an unavailable app in a folder says so, a cancelled folder
  reorder keeps its order, and Spotlight launches count once in recents.
- Discover no longer saves its own copy of Home.
- Five more strings are translated (Korean and Chinese are drafts for a native read).

## For anyone building Folio Dev

- An Island tester under Settings > General > Advanced > Developer fires made-up calls, timers, music and messages.

## Not in this beta

Widgets move apps, StandBy 2, Drop to Home, the Icon Actions picker, and the items held for the next beta (see Steps).

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **The island** on the cover and the unfolded screen: a call, a message, a timer; tap outside the card; swipe a call away.
2. **The Home button** from page 2 on Home, and from an app opened on page 2.
3. **App Library search:** type, open a result, come back; open the library and watch the tiles load.
4. **Clear Suggestions History,** then look at Spotlight and the Suggestions widget.
5. **Home never staying blurred:** open and close sheets, menus and folders, and install a new build over this one.
6. **A cancelled drag,** and a cancelled folder reorder.
7. **Backups:** restore one that has Smart Stacks.
8. **Safe Mode** past a minute, and a Focus turned off then restart.

Please say which phone, which screen (cover or inner, folded or open) and your Android version with each report.
