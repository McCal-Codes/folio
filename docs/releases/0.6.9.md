# Folio 0.6.9: smoother and refined more than ever

DRAFT, 31 Oct 2026. The stable 0.6.9. It is the app of `v0.6.9-beta.8` and nothing else: REL-4b, checked on this bump,
fails if anything that goes into the APK differs from that tag, apart from the version, the dated changelog and the
roadmap. The full list of what is new is the `## [0.6.9]` section of [CHANGELOG.md](../../CHANGELOG.md); the beta notes are
[0.6.9-beta.8](0.6.9-beta.8.md) and the betas before it. The opening paragraph of the public release notes is McCal's
and is not written here (REL-25).

## Version

`folioVersion = "0.6.9"`, `versionCode` **6090**, the same as every 0.6.9 beta, so it installs over beta.8 and the betas
before it, and over 0.6.8 (6080). The changelog's `[0.6.9]` is dated (REL-10). The roadmap marks every 0.6.9 item Done
(REL-14b); Private While Recording and Storage manager moved to 0.7.0, because they stay for supporters and Folio Dev
until then. Smoother swiping is marked Done for what shipped (less redrawing); the swipe itself is a 0.7.0 job.

## Before the bump

1. **Beta.8 is the last beta.** It is beta.6 plus the open gates, the Quick Settings tiles, the wallpaper work and seven
   fixes; beta.7 was tagged but never published. `v0.6.9-beta.8` is on the merge of the beta.8 bump (bde55b25).
2. Beta.8 was built and signed from a clean checkout of that tag; SHA-256 `a7dfc6c4...f41796`, signer
   `bad8e099...8441` (CN=McCal Media). The draft release on McCal-Codes/folio-beta was downloaded back and its checksum
   matched.
3. Not checked on a Fold8 yet: the inner screen, TalkBack, Reduce Motion, panel switch frame timing, Home spacing,
   a fresh install. McCal's call whether to soak beta.8 with supporters first (REL-20 asks for a day only for
   installer changes, and there are none).

## Steps

1. Publish beta.8 on McCal-Codes/folio-beta if it has not gone out (McCal's yes; this is the #beta post).
2. Merge this bump with a merge commit (McCal's yes).
3. Tag `v0.6.9` on it.
4. Build and sign from the tag, clean tree, the same script as the betas.
5. Upload the APK to VirusTotal (McCal's yes) and read the report.
6. Publish on McCal-Codes/folio as Latest. In the message: the APK's SHA-256 (REL-22), the VirusTotal report (REL-23)
   with the usual heads-up about small engines, and a pointer to [PERMISSIONS.md](../../PERMISSIONS.md) (REL-24); 18
   permissions, the same as beta.6. Release notes are in McCal's words (REL-25).
7. Check Settings > Software Update on a phone on 0.6.8 offers it, and that the website (`foliolauncher.com`) picks up
   the release.
