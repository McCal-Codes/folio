# Folio 0.6.9-beta.1 for supporters

DRAFT, 7 Oct 2026. Claude's wording from the changelog; McCal rewrites it before it goes anywhere (REL-25). Names and counts in
angle brackets are still to be filled in from what actually merges.

The first beta of 0.6.9. Version `0.6.9-beta.1`, versionCode 6090, so it installs over 0.6.8 (6080) and the stable 0.6.9
will install over it. Supporters get it through Market > Sources > Folio Beta.

## What's new

- **Focus that turns on by itself:** a Focus can turn on when the phone is unfolded, charging or has headphones, and turn
  off again when that stops. Turn it off yourself and it stays off until the signal changes.
- **Drag the dock in edit mode:** a handle under the Side Bar dock moves it up or down.
- **Bottom of Home:** choose a Search button, Page dots or Nothing above the dock. **Stronger rings** make the status rings
  thicker and clearer, and turn on with Android's Bold text.
- **Suggestions, any size** and **your own picture for one app's icon.**
- **Folders you can shape:** resize one by its corner, drop an app on another to make a folder, reorder inside it, and drag an app out past
  its edge to put it anywhere on Home, another page or the dock. Sort A to Z is in its options. Supporters only in this beta.
- **Make the Big Clock your own:** long-press it, then Customize for Looks (including Stacked), a color, 12 or 24-hour and the rest
  under Fine tune, or Place Freely to put it a part of a cell off the grid. Supporters only in this beta.
- **The Market keeps your sources current:** refresh in the background, update notices and automatic package updates, all
  off until you turn them on. The first time the Market opens it asks once.
- **System Bridge** (one switch for everything that reaches past a normal app), **Fold motion**, a **Performance log**,
  **Capabilities and Recent Activity** and **Copy Diagnostics** in Settings > Advanced.
- <the new setup, if it goes in: the short flow, a navigation page for Android users, and a quiet advanced step>
- Fixes: a scheduled Focus you turned off stays off, no alarm storm when the clocks go back, media controls without
  Notification access, less redrawing while apps install, and the Roadmap stops retrying offline.

The Korean and Chinese text of the new lines is Claude's translation and has not had a native review.

## What to try, most likely broken first

1. **Bottom of Home** (Settings > Home Screen & Dock): switch between Search button, Page dots and Nothing. Home should not
   shift, and dragging along the bottom should still change pages.
2. **Stronger rings** (Settings > Status): on the cover and inner screens, light and dark wallpaper; Android's Bold text turns it on.
3. **Focus triggers** (a Focus > Also Turn On When): unfold, plug in, connect headphones; turn it off by hand and see it stay off.
4. **Drag the dock** (edit Home, dock on the Side Bar): drag the handle; fold or unfold while editing.
5. **Market** (Market settings > Refreshing): nothing is on to start with; the question the first time you open it; turn
   refresh on, then a notice and an automatic update when a source has one.
6. **Safe Mode**: with it on, the Market refresh and staged updates do nothing.
7. **Installing an app** while Home is open, and **Wi-Fi off** with the Side Bar showing: Home should stay smooth.
8. **Korean or Chinese with TalkBack on**: Control Center toggles, the new Market question, the notices.
9. **Folders** (hold an app in an open folder and drag it past the edge; drop an app on a folder; resize by the corner): try it on the
   cover and the inner screen.
10. **Big Clock** (long-press > Customize and Place Freely): pick a Look, nudge it, put it back with Snap to Grid.
11. <the new setup, for anyone who can reach it>
